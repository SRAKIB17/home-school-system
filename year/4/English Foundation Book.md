# English Foundation Book & Early Phonics Reader — Age 4 (Beginner Level / KG-1)

## 1. Learning Philosophy & Objectives

### Philosophy & Vision
At age 4, children transition from single-sound awareness to fluent word blending, sight word automaticity, guided sentence reading, and confident spoken English communication. This textbook is designed with systematic synthetic phonics (Jolly Phonics & Orton-Gillingham aligned), 100+ CVC words, 40+ high-frequency sight words, beginning digraphs (`sh, ch, th, wh`), decodable mini-storybooks, 4-line handwriting guidelines, and Islamic character values.

### Core Learning Milestones
1. **Advanced Phonics & CVC Mastery:** Reading, decoding, and spelling 100+ short vowel words across `-at, -an, -ap, -et, -en, -ed, -in, -ip, -it, -og, -op, -ot, -un, -ug, -ut` families.
2. **Consonant Digraphs:** Auditory and visual mastery of `sh` (quiet ship), `ch` (choo-choo train), `th` (soft/hard thumb/the), and `wh` (whistling whale).
3. **Sight Words Automaticity:** Instant recognition of 40 Dolch Pre-Primer & Primer sight words.
4. **Decodable Storybooks:** Independent comprehension of 4–8 sentence stories with expressive intonation.
5. **Spoken English & Presentation:** Confident 1-minute self-introduction, requesting, gratitude, and feelings vocabulary.
6. **Handwriting Development:** Clean writing of uppercase and lowercase letters on 4-line ruled notebook pages.

## 2. 40-Week Comprehensive English Reader & Phonics Curriculum

### Term 1: Short 'a' & 'e' Word Families, Handwriting & Sight Words (Weeks 1–10)

---

#### Week 1 — Short 'a' -at & -an Families
* **Phonics Sound:** Short /æ/ blending.
* **-at Family:** c-a-t (**cat** 🐱), b-a-t (**bat** 🏏), h-a-t (**hat** 🎩), m-a-t (**mat** 🧘), r-a-t (**rat** 🐀), f-a-t (**fat** 🐖), s-a-t (**sat** 🪑).
* **-an Family:** p-a-n (**pan** 🍳), f-a-n (**fan** 🪭), m-a-n (**man** 👨), v-a-n (**van** 🚐), c-a-n (**can** 🥫), r-a-n (**ran** 🏃).
* **Target Sight Words:** **I**, **see**, **a**, **the**.
* **Decodable Story 1: "The Fat Cat"**
  > *I see a cat.*
  > *The cat is fat.*
  > *The fat cat has a hat.*
  > *The cat sat on the mat.*
* **Parent-Child Dialogue:** "Look at the fat cat! What is on its head? Yes, a red hat!"
* **4-Line Writing Practice:** Writing `cat, pan, man, hat` cleanly on ruled lines.

---

#### Week 2 — Short 'a' -ap & -ag Families
* **-ap Family:** c-a-p (**cap** 🧢), m-a-p (**map** 🗺️), t-a-p (**tap** 🚰), n-a-p (**nap** 💤), l-a-p (**lap** 👶).
* **-ag Family:** b-a-g (**bag** 🎒), t-a-g (**tag** 🏷️), r-a-g (**rag** 🧽), w-a-g (**wag** 🐕).
* **Target Sight Words:** **my**, **has**, **on**, **is**.
* **Decodable Story 2: "Sam's Bag"**
  > *Sam is a man.*
  > *Sam has a bag.*
  > *A map is in the bag.*
  > *Sam took a nap on my lap.*

---

#### Week 3 — Short 'a' -ad & -am Families + Sight Word Review
* **-ad Family:** d-a-d (**dad** 👨‍👧), s-a-d (**sad** 😢), m-a-d (**mad** 😠), b-a-d (**bad** 🚫).
* **-am Family:** j-a-m (**jam** 🍓), h-a-m (**ham** 🍖), r-a-m (**ram** 🐏).
* **Decodable Story 3: "Dad and Jam"**
  > *Dad has a red bag.*
  > *Dad has sweet jam.*
  > *Do not be sad, Dad is glad!*
* **Islamic Reflection:** "Dad works hard for us. Always smile and say: *JazakAllahu Khair, Dad!*"

---

#### Week 4 — Short 'e' -et & -en Families
* **Phonics Sound:** Short /e/ (as in *elephant*).
* **-et Family:** n-e-t (**net** 🕸️), p-e-t (**pet** 🐕), w-e-t (**wet** 💧), j-e-t (**jet** ✈️), s-e-t (**set** 📦).
* **-en Family:** h-e-n (**hen** 🐔), p-e-n (**pen** 🖊️), t-e-n (**ten** 🔟), m-e-n (**men** 👥).
* **Target Sight Words:** **he**, **she**, **it**, **and**.
* **Decodable Story 4: "Ten Red Hens"**
  > *Look at the pen.*
  > *I see ten hens.*
  > *A red hen is wet.*
  > *She is my pet hen.*

---

#### Week 5 — Short 'e' -ed & -eg Families
* **-ed Family:** b-e-d (**bed** 🛏️), r-e-d (**red** 🔴), f-e-d (**fed** 🥣), l-e-d (**led** 🚶).
* **-eg Family:** l-e-g (**leg** 🦵), p-e-g (**peg** 🪚), b-e-g (**beg** 🐶).
* **Decodable Story 5: "The Red Bed"**
  > *Ted fed his pet.*
  > *The pet is on the red bed.*
  > *Ted has a long leg.*

---

#### Week 6 — Short 'a' vs. Short 'e' Auditory Discrimination
* **Minimal Pairs Drills:**
  * `b-a-t` (bat) vs. `b-e-t` (bet)
  * `p-a-n` (pan) vs. `p-e-n` (pen)
  * `m-a-n` (man) vs. `m-e-n` (men)
  * `b-a-d` (bad) vs. `b-e-d` (bed)
* **Game:** Parent says a word; child steps on Letter A card or Letter E card on the floor.

---

#### Week 7 — Capitalization & Full Stop (. / ABC Rules)
* **Sentence Rules:**
  1. A sentence always starts with a **Capital Letter** (e.g., *The...*).
  2. Words must have **finger spaces** between them.
  3. A sentence ends with a **Full Stop (.)**.

---

#### Week 8 — Color Words & Number Words Reading
* **Color Words:** Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, Pink, Black, White.
* **Number Words (1–10):** One, Two, Three, Four, Five, Six, Seven, Eight, Nine, Ten.
* **Reading Practice:** "I have **three** **red** caps and **two** **blue** pens."

---

#### Week 9 — Spoken English Module 1: Self-Introduction
* **Script for Child:**
  > *"As-Salamu Alaykum!*
  > *My name is [Child's Name].*
  > *I am 4 years old.*
  > *I live with my loving family.*
  > *I love to read books and draw stars.*
  > *Thank you!"*

---

* **-ig Family:** b-i-g (**big** 🐘), p-i-g (**pig** 🐷), d-i-g (**dig** ⛏️), f-i-g (**fig** 🫐), w-i-g (**wig** 💇).

#### Week 14 — Short 'o' -og & -ox Families

---

#### Week 15 — Consonant Digraph: sh /ʃ/ (The Quiet Sound)
* **Sound & Gesture:** Put index finger on lips: "Shhh, be quiet!"
* **Words:** **sh**ip 🚢, **sh**op 🏬, **sh**oe 👞, **sh**ed 🛖, fi**sh** 🐟, di**sh** 🍽️, wa**sh** 🧼.
* **Story:** "I wash my dish with clean water. I see a big ship in the shop."

---

#### Week 16 — Consonant Digraph: ch /tʃ/ (The Train Sound)
* **Sound & Gesture:** Chug arms like train wheels: "Ch-ch-choo-choo!"
* **Words:** **ch**ip 🍟, **ch**in 🧔, **ch**op 🪓, **ch**at 💬, ri**ch** 💎, mu**ch** 💰.
* **Story:** "Chop the wood! Touch your chin. Let us chat and eat a chip."

---

#### Week 17 — Consonant Digraph: th /θ/ & /ð/ (Tongue Between Teeth)
* **Soft /θ/:** **th**in 📏, **th**ick 📚, **th**umb 👍, **th**ink 🧠.
* **Hard /ð/:** **th**is 👈, **th**at 👉, **th**e 🌟, **th**ey 👥, **th**em.
* **Practice:** Touch thumb and say: "This is my thumb."

---

#### Week 18 — Consonant Digraph: wh /w/ (The Whistling Wind)
* **Words:** **wh**ale 🐋, **wh**eel 🛞, **wh**ite ⚪, **wh**at ❓, **wh**en ⏰, **wh**ere 📍.
* **Question Drills:** "What is this?" -> "This is a white wheel."

---

#### Week 19 — Sight Words Speed Challenge (Group 2)
* Target Words: *all, are, out, come, here, look, said, you, was, with, they, have*.

### Term 4: Guided Storybooks, Creative Writing & Public Speaking (Weeks 31–40)

---

#### Week 31 — Guided Storybook 1: "The Brave Little Ant"
* **Full Text:**
  > *Once there was a little black ant.*
  > *The ant saw a sweet big nut.*
  > *The nut was under a green leaf.*
  > *The ant said: "Bismillah!" and pushed the nut.*
  > *All the ants helped. They had a big feast!*
* **Comprehension Questions:**
  1. What did the little ant see? (A sweet big nut).
  2. Where was the nut? (Under a green leaf).
  3. What did the ant say before pushing? (Bismillah!).

---

#### Week 32 — Guided Storybook 2: "A Rainy Day Blessing"
