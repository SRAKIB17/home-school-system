# English Foundation Book & Phonics — Age 4 (Beginner Level)

## 1. Learning Objectives
* Master short vowel CVC word families (100+ words).
* Recognize 40+ Dolch pre-primer sight words instantly.
* Read and understand 3–5 word decodable sentences independently.
* Write clean uppercase and lowercase letters in 4-line notebooks.
* Express basic needs, feelings, and greetings in spoken English.

---

## 2. Comprehensive CVC Word Families

### Short 'a' Families
* **-at:** cat, bat, hat, rat, mat, fat, sat.
* **-an:** pan, fan, man, van, can, ran.
* **-ap:** cap, map, tap, nap, lap.
* **-ag:** bag, tag, rag, wag.
* **-ad:** dad, sad, mad, bad.

### Short 'e' Families
* **-et:** net, pet, wet, jet, set.
* **-en:** hen, pen, ten, men.
* **-ed:** bed, red, fed.

### Short 'i' Families
* **-in:** pin, bin, win, tin.
* **-ip:** lip, tip, sip, dip.
* **-it:** sit, fit, hit, kit.
* **-ig:** big, pig, dig, fig.

### Short 'o' Families
* **-ot:** hot, pot, dot, lot.
* **-op:** top, mop, hop, pop.
* **-og:** dog, log, fog, frog.

### Short 'u' Families
* **-un:** sun, run, bun, fun.
* **-ug:** bug, mug, jug, rug.
* **-ut:** nut, hut, cut.

## 3. High-Frequency Sight Words & Decodable Stories

### Sight Words List (40 Words)
* I, a, the, see, can, my, like, this, is, you, we, in, on, at, go, to, no, he, she, it, has, had, have, and, look, said, come, here, big, little, red, blue, one, two, three, play, jump, help, make, funny.

### Decodable Stories
1. **The Fat Cat:**
   * "I see a cat. The cat is fat. The fat cat sat on a red mat. The cat has a big hat."
2. **Sam and his Dog:**
   * "This is Sam. Sam has a dog. The dog is big. The dog can run in the hot sun."
3. **The Red Hen:**
   * "Look at the hen. The hen is red. The red hen is in the pen. She has ten eggs."

---

## 4. Beginning Blends & Digraphs Intro
* **sh- /ʃ/:** ship, shop, shoe, fish, dish.
* **ch- /tʃ/:** chip, chin, chair, rich.
* **th- /θ/:** thin, think, thumb, with.
* **wh- /w/:** whale, wheel, white.

---

## 5. Spoken English Dialogue & Public Speaking
* **Self-Introduction:**
  * "Hello! My name is Bilal. I am 4 years old. I like to read books and draw trees."
* **Polite Daily Expressions:**
  * "May I come in, please?"
  * "Excuse me, where is my book?"
  * "Thank you so much!"
  * "Have a wonderful day!"
