# English Focus — Age 4 (Beginner Level)

## Learning Objectives
* **Age:** 4 Years
* **Core Competencies:**
  1. Full Phonics Mastery (Short vowels a, e, i, o, u & beginning blends).
  2. Fluent CVC word reading and writing (100+ words).
  3. Sight Words Recognition (40+ Dolch pre-primer sight words).
  4. Reading & comprehending short 3–5 word decodable sentences.
  5. Neat English handwriting in 4-line notebooks.
  6. Confident spoken English dialogues for daily routines.

---

## 40-Week Structured Curriculum

### Term 1: Short Vowel Families (CVC Words) (Weeks 1–10)
* **Week 1 — Short 'a' Families (-at, -an):** cat, bat, hat, rat, mat / pan, fan, man, van.
* **Week 2 — Short 'a' Families (-ap, -ag, -ad):** cap, map, tap / bag, tag, rag / dad, sad, mad.
* **Week 3 — Short 'e' Families (-et, -en, -ed):** net, pet, wet / hen, pen, ten / bed, red, fed.
* **Week 4 — Short 'i' Families (-in, -ip, -it):** pin, bin, win / lip, tip, sip / sit, fit, hit.
* **Week 5 — Short 'i' Families (-ig, -id):** big, pig, dig / kid, lid.
* **Week 6 — Short 'o' Families (-ot, -op, -og):** hot, pot, dot / top, mop, hop / dog, log, frog.
* **Week 7 — Short 'u' Families (-un, -ug, -ut):** sun, run, bun / bug, mug, jug / nut, hut, cut.
* **Week 8 — CVC Sound Blending & Segmenting:** Dictation & Spelling games.
* **Week 9 — Handwriting in 4-Line Notebook:** Uppercase & Lowercase letters Aa–Zz neat drill.
* **Week 10 — Term 1 Assessment & Phonics Fun Quiz.**

### Term 2: Sight Words & Early Sentence Reading (Weeks 11–20)
* **Week 11 — High Frequency Sight Words 1:** I, a, the, see, is, can. (Reading: "I see a red cat.").
* **Week 12 — Sight Words 2:** my, like, this, is, you, we. (Reading: "This is my dog. We like to play.").
* **Week 13 — Sight Words 3:** in, on, at, go, to, no. (Reading: "The cat is on the mat.").
* **Week 14 — Sight Words 4:** he, she, it, has, had, have. (Reading: "He has a big bag.").
* **Week 15 — Reading Simple Sentences 1:** 
  * "A fat cat sat on a mat."
  * "The sun is hot and big."
* **Week 16 — Reading Simple Sentences 2:**
  * "I have a pet dog."
  * "The dog can run and hop."
* **Week 17 — Opposites in English:** Hot/Cold, Fast/Slow, Big/Small, Up/Down, In/Out, Happy/Sad.
* **Week 18 — Days of the Week in English:** Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday.
* **Week 19 — Months of the Year:** January to December rhyme.
* **Week 20 — Mid-Year Reading & Spelling Evaluation.**

### Term 3: Consonant Blends, Digraphs & Writing Sentences (Weeks 21–30)
* **Week 21 — Beginning Blends (sh, ch):** 
  * /ʃ/ as in ship, shop, shoe, fish / /tʃ/ as in chip, chin, chair.
* **Week 22 — Digraphs (th, wh):** 
  * /θ/ as in thin, think, thumb / /w/ as in whale, wheel, white.
* **Week 23 — Double Consonants (ll, ss, ff):** bell, ball, doll / grass, kiss / puff, cliff.
* **Week 24 — Sentence Building Practice:** Arranging jumbled words (e.g., [is / a / It / pen] -> "It is a pen.").
* **Week 25 — Writing My Own Sentences (3 words):** 
  * "I love Allah."
  * "I see trees."
  * "I am happy."
* **Week 26 — Punctuation Basics:** Capital letter at the beginning & Full stop (.) at the end.
* **Week 27 — Guided Story Reading 1:** "Sam and his Big Van" (Decodable story).
* **Week 28 — Guided Story Reading 2:** "The Red Hen in the Pen".
* **Week 29 — Answering "What" & "Who" questions from short stories.**
* **Week 30 — Vocabulary Expansion: Occupations, Animals & Vehicles.**

### Term 4: Spoken English, Dialogue & Final Mastery (Weeks 31–40)
* **Week 31 — Self-Introduction in English:**
  * "Hello! My name is ... I am 4 years old. I like to draw and read books."
* **Week 32 — Expressing Feelings & Needs:**
  * "I am hungry / thirsty / sleepy."
  * "May I go to the washroom, please?"
* **Week 33 — Courteous Manners in English:**
  * "Excuse me", "Pardon me", "Thank you very much", "Have a good day!"
* **Week 34 — Daily Objects & Prepositions:**
  * The book is **on** the table. The ball is **under** the chair.
* **Week 35 — Action Song Recitation:** "If you're happy and you know it clap your hands".
* **Week 36 — Picture Description (2 minutes speech):** Holding a toy and talking about its color, size, and function.
* **Week 37 — English Dictation (10 Sight Words + 5 CVC Sentences).**
* **Week 38 — Comprehensive Phonics & Reading Review.**
* **Week 39 — Annual Spoken & Written Evaluation.**
* **Week 40 — "Confident Young Reader" Certificate & Celebration.**
