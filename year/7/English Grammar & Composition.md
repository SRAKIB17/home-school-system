# English Grammar & Composition — Age 7 (Class 2 / Primary Advanced)

## Core Learning Objectives
* **Age:** 7 Years
* **Target Competencies:**
  1. Master Grade 2 English Grammar (All 8 Parts of Speech, Tenses: Simple Present/Past/Future & Continuous forms).
  2. Fluent reading of chapter books (Guided Reading Levels J–N / Lexile 300L–550L).
  3. Reading Comprehension: Critical thinking, inferring character motives, summarizing, and vocabulary in context.
  4. Composition: Writing structured 2–3 paragraph essays (80–120 words), descriptive narratives, and friendly letters.
  5. Public Speaking & Debate: Presenting speeches (3–5 minutes), reciting poetry with modulation, and discussing topics logically.

---

## 40-Week Structured Curriculum

### Term 1: Advanced Grammar & Sentence Mechanics (Weeks 1–10)
* **Week 1 — Nouns In-Depth:** Common, Proper, Collective (flock of birds, herd of cattle, bouquet of flowers) & Abstract (kindness, bravery, honesty).
* **Week 2 — Irregular Plural Nouns:** leaf -> leaves, wolf -> wolves, child -> children, mouse -> mice, foot -> feet.
* **Week 3 — Possessive Nouns ('s vs. s'):** "The boy's book" vs. "The boys' school".
* **Week 4 — Pronouns Review:** Subject, Object (me, him, her, us, them) & Demonstrative (this, that, these, those).
* **Week 5 — Action Verbs & Helping Verbs:** Main verbs vs. Auxiliary verbs (is, am, are, was, were, has, have, had, do, does, did).
* **Week 6 — Simple Present Tense:** Routine habits, universal truths ("The sun rises in the east").
* **Week 7 — Simple Past Tense (Regular & Irregular Verbs):** play -> played, visit -> visited / go -> went, see -> saw, buy -> bought, write -> wrote.
* **Week 8 — Simple Future Tense (Will / Shall + Verb):** "I will visit my grandparents tomorrow."
* **Week 9 — Present & Past Continuous Tense:** "They are playing football", "She was reading a book".
* **Week 10 — Term 1 Grammar Assessment & Mastery Exam.**

### Term 2: Adverbs, Prepositions, Conjunctions & Punctuation (Weeks 11–20)
* **Week 11 — Adjectives In-Depth:** Degrees of Comparison (Positive, Comparative, Superlative: fast, faster, fastest / good, better, best).
* **Week 12 — Adverbs (How, Where, When):** Adverbs of manner (slowly, loudly, carefully), place (everywhere, inside), time (yesterday, now).
* **Week 13 — Prepositions of Place, Direction & Time:** across the road, through the tunnel, into the room, during the night.
* **Week 14 — Conjunctions & Compound Sentences:** joining sentences using coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS: for, and, nor, but, or, yet, so).
* **Week 15 — Punctuation Mastery:** Capital letters, full stops (.), commas in lists (,), question marks (?), exclamation marks (!), quotation marks (" ").
* **Week 16 — Direct Speech & Dialogue Writing:** "Hello," said Bilal. "How are you today?"
* **Week 17 — Prefixes & Suffixes:** un- (unhappy), re- (rewrite), dis- (dislike) / -ful (careful), -less (careless), -ly (quickly).
* **Week 18 — Synonyms & Antonyms (Advanced 50 Pairs):** ancient/modern, gigantic/tiny, courageous/cowardly, furious/calm.
* **Week 19 — Idioms & Expressions:** "Piece of cake" (very easy), "Break the ice" (start conversation), "Once in a blue moon" (rarely).
* **Week 20 — Mid-Year Grammar & Vocabulary Championship.**

### Term 3: Reading Comprehension & Literature Appreciation (Weeks 21–30)
* **Week 21 — Reading Comprehension 1 (Historical Fiction):** Story of Salahuddin Ayyubi and the Crusader king.
* **Week 22 — Reading Comprehension 2 (Informational Text / Science):** How honeybees communicate through the waggle dance.
* **Week 23 — Reading Comprehension 3 (Biography):** The inspiring life of Thomas Edison / Ibn al-Haytham (Father of Optics).
* **Week 24 — Reading Comprehension 4 (Classic Fable):** Analyzing morals, conflict, and character traits.
* **Week 25 — Summarizing Texts:** Finding Main Idea + Supporting Details using the 5-Finger Retell Strategy.
* **Week 26 — Cause & Effect Analysis:** Identifying why things happened in a story.
* **Week 27 — Fact vs. Opinion:** Distinguishing objective statements from personal feelings.
* **Week 28 — Poetry Analysis & Recitation:** "Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening" by Robert Frost.
* **Week 29 — Independent Chapter Book Reading & Book Report Writing:** Title, Author, Setting, Plot Summary, Rating (⭐).
* **Week 30 — Term 3 Reading Comprehension Exam.**

### Term 4: Creative Composition, Letters & Public Speaking (Weeks 31–40)
* **Week 31 — Descriptive Essay Writing (100 words):** "My Favorite Place in Nature" (Using 5 sensory words: sight, sound, smell, touch, taste).
* **Week 32 — Narrative Story Writing (3 paragraphs):** 
  * Paragraph 1: Introduction (Characters & Setting).
  * Paragraph 2: Problem & Climax.
  * Paragraph 3: Resolution & Moral Lesson.
* **Week 33 — Persuasive Writing (Opinion Essay):** "Why We Should Plant More Trees in Our Neighborhood".
* **Week 34 — Friendly Letter Writing:** Writing a 3-paragraph letter to a pen pal about my daily life.
* **Week 35 — Formal Email / Note Writing:** Writing a polite request letter to a librarian or teacher.
* **Week 36 — Public Speaking / Speech (3–5 minutes):** "The Importance of Truthfulness in Everyday Life".
* **Week 37 — English Debate Competition:** "Should children have fixed screen time limits?" (Presenting logical arguments).
* **Week 38 — Advanced Dictation & Spelling Bee (50 challenging words + 2 complex paragraphs).**
* **Week 39 — Annual English Language & Literature Assessment.**
* **Week 40 — "Master of English Eloquence" Trophy & Certification.**
