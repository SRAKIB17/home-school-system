# English Focus — Age 5 (Junior Level)

## Learning Objectives
* **Age:** 5 Years
* **Target Skills:**
  1. Long Vowel Sounds (Silent 'e' rule & vowel teams like ee, ea, ai, oa).
  2. 100+ High-Frequency Sight Words (Dolch Primer & First Grade words).
  3. Basic Grammar: Nouns (Naming words), Verbs (Action words), Adjectives (Describing words).
  4. Reading leveled storybooks (Lexile BR–200L) independently with comprehension.
  5. Writing simple 3–5 sentence paragraphs with correct capitalization and punctuation.
  6. Conversational fluency in everyday social and family scenarios.

---

## 40-Week Curriculum Plan

### Term 1: Long Vowels & Magic 'e' Rule (Weeks 1–10)
* **Week 1 — Magic 'e' (Long a_e):** cap -> cape, tap -> tape, mad -> made, cake, bake, lake.
* **Week 2 — Magic 'e' (Long i_e):** pin -> pine, kit -> kite, bite, ride, time, like.
* **Week 3 — Magic 'e' (Long o_e & u_e):** hop -> hope, rob -> robe, home, bone / cub -> cube, tube, cute.
* **Week 4 — Vowel Teams (ee, ea):** see, bee, tree, green / sea, tea, leaf, read, eat.
* **Week 5 — Vowel Teams (ai, ay):** rain, train, tail, wait / day, play, say, pray.
* **Week 6 — Vowel Teams (oa, ow):** boat, coat, road, soap / snow, blow, grow, window.
* **Week 7 — R-Controlled Vowels (ar, or, ur/ir/er):** car, star, park / corn, fork / bird, girl, nurse, teacher.
* **Week 8 — Diphthongs (ou, ow, oi, oy):** out, shout, house / cow, clown / coin, point / toy, boy.
* **Week 9 — Spelling & Dictation Drills:** Writing long vowel words correctly in 4-line notebook.
* **Week 10 — Term 1 Assessment & Phonics Championship.**

### Term 2: Grammar Foundations & Sentence Structure (Weeks 11–20)
* **Week 11 — Nouns (Naming Words):** Person (boy, mother), Place (school, park), Thing (book, table), Animal (cat, bird).
* **Week 12 — Singular and Plural Nouns:** Adding -s and -es (cat -> cats, box -> boxes, dish -> dishes).
* **Week 13 — Pronouns:** He, She, It, They, We, You, I. (Replacing nouns in sentences).
* **Week 14 — Verbs (Action Words):** run, jump, read, write, eat, drink, pray, smile.
* **Week 15 — Helping Verbs (is, am, are):** "I am a boy", "He is kind", "They are playing".
* **Week 16 — Adjectives (Describing Words):** big, small, red, sweet, hot, kind, beautiful.
* **Week 17 — Prepositions:** in, on, under, near, behind, between.
* **Week 18 — Articles (A, An, The):** "A dog", "An apple", "The sun".
* **Week 19 — Conjunctions (and, but):** "I like apples and bananas."
* **Week 20 — Mid-Year Grammar Quiz & Evaluation.**

### Term 3: Reading Comprehension & Paragraph Writing (Weeks 21–30)
* **Week 21 — Reading Comprehension 1 (The Helpful Boy):** Reading 50 words story and answering who/what/where.
* **Week 22 — Reading Comprehension 2 (A Rainy Day):** Finding main idea and answering simple questions.
* **Week 23 — Reading Comprehension 3 (Animals in the Forest):** Animal habitats and habits.
* **Week 24 — Sentence Capitalization & Full Stop Rules:** "My name is Bilal."
* **Week 25 — Guided Writing: My Family (4 sentences):**
  * "This is my family. My father is kind. My mother cooks yummy food. I love my brother."
* **Week 26 — Guided Writing: My Favorite Toy (4 sentences).**
* **Week 27 — Guided Writing: My Daily Routine (4 sentences).**
* **Week 28 — Picture Composition:** Looking at a park picture and writing 4 descriptive sentences.
* **Week 29 — Creative Sentence Building:** Using word banks to compose colorful sentences.
* **Week 30 — Term 3 Writing & Reading Speed Assessment.**

### Term 4: Spoken Fluency, Public Speaking & Celebration (Weeks 31–40)
* **Week 31 — Conversational English: At the Market / Grocery Store:**
  * "How much is this book?", "Can I have two oranges, please?"
* **Week 32 — Conversational English: Meeting New Friends:**
  * "What is your name?", "Would you like to play with me?", "Nice to meet you!"
* **Week 33 — Telephone Manners in English:**
  * "Hello! May I speak to Uncle, please?", "Hold on a second, please."
* **Week 34 — Story Retelling in English:** Retelling the story of "The Tortoise and the Hare" in own words.
* **Week 35 — 2-Minute Speech (Public Speaking):** "Why I Love Prophet Muhammad (pbuh)".
* **Week 36 — English Rhyme & Poetry Recitation with Expression.**
* **Week 37 — Dictation Mastery (Paragraph of 25 words).**
* **Week 38 — Comprehensive Phonics, Grammar & Vocabulary Master Review.**
* **Week 39 — Annual Spoken & Written English Assessment.**
* **Week 40 — "Young Scholar of English" Award & Certification.**
