# English Reading & Grammar Master Book — Age 5 (Junior Level / KG-2 / Prep)

## 1. Learning Philosophy & Objectives

### Philosophy & Vision
At age 5 (Prep / KG-2), children advance into independent early reading, understanding complex phonetic structures (Magic 'e' silent vowels, vowel teams, diphthongs, R-controlled vowels), foundational grammar (Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Pronouns, Prepositions, Articles `a/an/the`), leveled reading comprehension stories, and expressive 4-to-5 sentence paragraph writing.

### Core Learning Milestones
1. **Long Vowels & Vowel Teams:** Mastery of Silent 'e' (`a_e, i_e, o_e, u_e`), Vowel Digraphs (`ee, ea, ai, ay, oa, ow, oi, oy, ou, oo`), and Bossy 'r' (`ar, er, ir, or, ur`).
2. **100+ High-Frequency Sight Words:** Instant fluency with Dolch Primer and 1st Grade sight words.
3. **Core English Grammar:**
   * Nouns (Common vs. Proper Nouns, Singular vs. Plural `-s/-es`).
   * Pronouns (`I, He, She, It, We, They, You`).
   * Action Verbs & Helping Verbs (`is, am, are, was, were, has, have`).
   * Adjectives (Colors, sizes, shapes, feelings, touch).
   * Articles (`a` vs. `an` vs. `the`).
   * Prepositions (`in, on, under, behind, in front of, between`).
4. **Reading Comprehension:** Reading 8–12 sentence moral and scientific stories with question-answering ability.
5. **Creative Paragraph Writing:** Structuring short 4-sentence paragraphs on familiar topics (*My Pet, My Mother, Rainy Day, The Garden*).
6. **Spoken English & Presentation:** Fluent conversation, asking questions, reciting poems, and 2-minute public speaking.

## 2. 40-Week Comprehensive English Reader & Grammar Curriculum

### Term 1: Long Vowels (Magic 'e'), Nouns & Sight Words (Weeks 1–10)

---

#### Week 1 — The Magic 'e' Rule: Long 'a_e' (Silent 'e' Magic)
* **Phonics Concept:** The silent 'e' at the end of a word jumps over the consonant and makes the vowel say its **own name**!
* **Transformation Drills:**
  * c - a - p (cap 🧢) + e -> **cape** 🦸
  * t - a - p (tap 🚰) + e -> **tape** 🩹
  * m - a - d (mad 😠) + e -> **made** 🔨
  * r - a - t (rat 🐀) + e -> **rate** 📈
* **Word Family:** c-a-k-e (**cake** 🎂), b-a-k-e (**bake** 🥧), l-a-k-e (**lake** 🏞️), n-a-m-e (**name** 🏷️), g-a-m-e (**game** 🎮), s-a-f-e (**safe** 🛡️).
* **Decodable Story 1: "The Red Cape"**
  > *Jake has a red cape.*
  > *Jake went to the lake.*
  > *Mom will bake a sweet cake.*
  > *They play a fun game by the lake.*

---

#### Week 2 — Long 'i_e' (Silent 'e')
* **Transformation Drills:**
  * p - i - n (pin 📌) + e -> **pine** 🌲
  * k - i - t (kit 🧰) + e -> **kite** 🪁
  * r - i - d (rid) + e -> **ride** 🚲
  * b - i - t (bit 🥪) + e -> **bite** 🍏
* **Vocabulary:** t-i-m-e (**time** ⏰), l-i-k-e (**like** 👍), f-i-v-e (**five** 5️⃣), n-i-n-e (**nine** 9️⃣), w-h-i-t-e (**white** ⚪), s-m-i-l-e (**smile** 😊).
* **Decodable Story 2: "Mike and his Kite"**
  > *Mike has a white kite.*
  > *Mike likes to ride his bike.*
  > *It is five o'clock in the afternoon.*
  > *Mike has a big, happy smile.*

---

#### Week 3 — Long 'o_e' and Long 'u_e'
* **Long 'o_e':** h-o-p (hop 🐇) + e -> **hope** 🌟, r-o-b (rob) + e -> **robe** 👘, h-o-m-e (**home** 🏠), b-o-n-e (**bone** 🦴), r-o-s-e (**rose** 🌹), n-o-s-e (**nose** 👃), s-t-o-n-e (**stone** 🪨).
* **Long 'u_e':** c-u-b (cub 🐻) + e -> **cube** 🎲, t-u-b-e (**tube** 🧪), c-u-t-e (**cute** 🐱), m-u-t-e (**mute** 🔇), h-u-g-e (**huge** 🐘).
* **Decodable Story 3: "The Cute Puppy at Home"**
  > *Rose is at home.*
  > *She has a cute little puppy.*
  > *The puppy plays with a bone.*
  > *A huge box is by the stone.*

---

#### Week 4 — Grammar: Nouns (Naming Words — Common & Proper)
* **What is a Noun?** A noun is the name of a **Person**, **Place**, **Animal**, or **Thing**.
* **Common Nouns (General names):** boy, girl, city, river, book, cat.
* **Proper Nouns (Special names — Always Capitalized!):** Bilal, Aisha, Dhaka, Quran, Bangladesh.
* **Activity:** Circle Common Nouns with blue crayon and Proper Nouns with green crayon.

---

#### Week 5 — Grammar: Singular & Plural Nouns (Adding -s and -es)
* **Rule 1 (Add -s):** book -> books, pen -> pens, apple -> apples, tree -> trees.
* **Rule 2 (Add -es when words end in -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o):**
  * bus -> bus**es** 🚌
  * glass -> glass**es** 🥛
  * dish -> dish**es** 🍽️
  * watch -> watch**es** ⌚
  * box -> box**es** 📦
  * tomato -> tomato**es** 🍅

---

#### Week 6 — Grammar: Articles — 'a' vs. 'an'
* **Rule for 'an':** Use **an** before words that start with a vowel sound (**a, e, i, o, u**).
  * *an* apple 🍎, *an* elephant 🐘, *an* igloo 🧊, *an* orange 🍊, *an* umbrella ☂️.
* **Rule for 'a':** Use **a** before words that start with consonant sounds.
  * *a* boy 👦, *a* cat 🐱, *a* book 📖, *a* house 🏠, *a* tree 🌳.
* **Drill:** Fill in the blanks with *a* or *an*: `___ egg`, `___ chair`, `___ airplane`, `___ mango`.

---

#### Week 7 — Sight Words Speed Mastery (Dolch Primer List 1)
* Target Words: *all, am, are, at, ate, be, black, brown, but, came, did, do, eat, four, get, good, have, he, into, like, must, new, no, now, on, our, out, please, pretty, ran, ride, saw, say, she, so, soon, that, there, they, this, too, under, want, was, well, went, what, white, who, will, with, yes*.

---

#### Week 8 — Leveled Comprehension Story 1: "The Generous Prophet ﷺ"
* **Text:**
  > *Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was the kindest man in the world.*
  > *He always spoke the truth.*
  > *He smiled at everyone and helped the poor.*
  > *He loved little children and stroked their heads.*
  > *We love our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.*
* **Comprehension Questions:**
  1. Who was the kindest man? (Prophet Muhammad ﷺ).
  2. What did he always speak? (The truth).
  3. How did he treat little children? (He loved them and stroked their heads).

---

#### Week 9 — Writing: Sentence Construction & Punctuation
* **Sentence Formula:** Capital letter at start + Finger spacing + Subject + Verb + Full stop / Question mark (`. / ?`).
* Example: *The green frog can jump high.*

### Term 2: Vowel Teams, Diphthongs, Pronouns & Verbs (Weeks 11–20)

---

#### Week 11 — Vowel Teams: 'ee' and 'ea' (Long 'e' Sound /iː/)
* **'ee' Words:** s-e-e (**see** 👀), b-e-e (**bee** 🐝), t-r-e-e (**tree** 🌳), g-r-e-e-n (**green** 🟢), f-e-e-t (**feet** 🦶), s-l-e-e-p (**sleep** 😴).
* **'ea' Words:** s-e-a (**sea** 🌊), t-e-a (**tea** ☕), l-e-a-f (**leaf** 🍃), r-e-a-d (**read** 📖), e-a-t (**eat** 🍽️), c-l-e-a-n (**clean** 🧼).
* **Decodable Story 4: "The Green Tree by the Sea"**
  > *I see a green tree.*
  > *The green tree is near the blue sea.*
  > *A little bee is on the green leaf.*
  > *The bee likes sweet tea.*

---

#### Week 12 — Vowel Teams: 'ai' and 'ay' (Long 'a' Sound /eɪ/)
* **'ai' Words (Middle of word):** r-a-i-n (**rain** 🌧️), t-r-a-i-n (**train** 🚂), t-a-i-l (**tail** 🐕), w-a-i-t (**wait** ⏳), s-a-i-l (**sail** ⛵).
* **'ay' Words (End of word):** d-a-y (**day** ☀️), p-l-a-y (**play** 🛝), s-a-y (**say** 🗣️), p-r-a-y (**pray** 🤲), m-a-y (**may** 🌸).
* **Decodable Story 5: "A Rainy Day"**
  > *Look at the rain!*
  > *The train is on the track.*
  > *We will wait for the rain to stop.*
  > *Then we will go out and play today.*

---

#### Week 13 — Vowel Teams: 'oa' and 'ow' (Long 'o' Sound /oʊ/)
* **'oa' Words:** b-o-a-t (**boat** ⛵), c-o-a-t (**coat** 🧥), r-o-a-d (**road** 🛣️), s-o-a-p (**soap** 🧼), g-o-a-t (**goat** 🐐).
* **'ow' Words (Long o):** s-n-o-w (**snow** ❄️), b-l-o-w (**blow** 💨), g-r-o-w (**grow** 🌱), s-l-o-w (**slow** 🐢), w-i-n-d-o-w (**window** 🪟).
* **Decodable Story 6: "The Goat in the Boat"**
  > *A white goat is in the boat.*
  > *The goat wears a warm coat.*
  > *The boat sails down the road.*
  > *Wash your hands with clean soap!*

---

#### Week 14 — Grammar: Pronouns (I, He, She, It, We, They, You)
* Replace the underlined nouns with correct pronouns:
  * *Ahmad* is reading. -> **He** is reading.
  * *Fatima* is smiling. -> **She** is smiling.
  * *The cat* is sleeping. -> **It** is sleeping.
  * *Bilal and I* are praying. -> **We** are praying.
  * *The children* are playing. -> **They** are playing.

---

#### Week 15 — Grammar: Action Verbs & Subject-Verb Agreement
* **Action Verbs:** run, walk, read, write, swim, jump, fly, pray, smile.
* **Adding -s / -es for singular subjects:**
  * *He* play**s** football. / *She* read**s** Quran.
  * *They* play football. / *I* read Quran.

---

#### Week 16 — Grammar: Helping Verbs (is, am, are)
* **am:** Always with **I** (*I am happy.*)
* **is:** With singular subjects (**He, She, It, This, Bilal**) (*He is kind. It is a ball.*)
* **are:** With plural subjects (**We, They, You, These**) (*We are students. You are nice.*)

---

#### Week 17 — Diphthongs: 'ou' and 'ow' (/aʊ/ as in *Ouch!*)
* **'ou' Words:** o-u-t (**out** 🚪), s-h-o-u-t (**shout** 📢), h-o-u-s-e (**house** 🏠), m-o-u-s-e (**mouse** 🐁), c-l-o-u-d (**cloud** ☁️).
* **'ow' Words:** c-o-w (**cow** 🐄), c-l-o-w-n (**clown** 🤡), t-o-w-n (**town** 🏙️), b-r-o-w-n (**brown** 🟤), d-o-w-n (**down** ⬇️).
* **Decodable Story 7: "The Brown Cow"**
  > *Look at the big brown cow!*
  > *The cow is near the white house.*
  > *A dark cloud is in the sky.*
  > *Do not shout, come in the house!*

---

#### Week 18 — Diphthongs: 'oi' and 'oy' (/ɔɪ/ as in *Coin/Boy*)
* **'oi' Words:** c-o-i-n (**coin** 🪙), b-o-i-l (**boil** ♨️), s-o-i-l (**soil** 🌱), p-o-i-n-t (**point** ☝️).
* **'oy' Words:** b-o-y (**boy** 👦), t-o-y (**toy** 🧸), j-o-y (**joy** 😃).
* **Story:** "The little boy has a new toy. He plants a seed in the rich soil."

---

#### Week 19 — Double 'oo': Short /ʊ/ vs. Long /uː/
* **Short 'oo' (/ʊ/ as in book):** b-o-o-k (**book** 📖), l-o-o-k (**look** 👀), f-o-o-t (**foot** 🦶), c-o-o-k (**cook** 👨‍🍳), w-o-o-d (**wood** 🪵).
* **Long 'oo' (/uː/ as in moon):** m-o-o-n (**moon** 🌙), s-p-o-o-n (**spoon** 🥄), f-o-o-d (**food** 🍲), z-o-o (**zoo** 🦁), c-o-o-l (**cool** ❄️).

---

### Term 3: Bossy 'r', Adjectives, Prepositions & Paragraphs (Weeks 21–30)

---

#### Week 21 — Bossy 'r' / R-Controlled Vowels: 'ar' and 'or'
* **'ar' (/ɑːr/ as in star):** c-a-r (**car** 🚗), s-t-a-r (**star** ⭐), p-a-r-k (**park** 🏞️), b-a-r-n (**barn** 🚜), f-a-r-m (**farm** 🧑‍🌾), h-e-a-r-t (**heart** ❤️).
* **'or' (/ɔːr/ as in fork):** f-o-r-k (**fork** 🍴), c-o-r-n (**corn** 🌽), h-o-r-n (**horn** 📯), s-t-o-r-m (**storm** ⛈️), m-o-r-n-i-n-g (**morning** 🌅).
* **Decodable Story 8: "The Farm in the Morning"**
  > *It is a bright morning.*
  > *We go to the big farm in our car.*
  > *We see a yellow star in the sky.*
  > *The horse eats sweet corn in the barn.*

---

#### Week 22 — Bossy 'r': 'er', 'ir', 'ur' (The /ɜːr/ Sound)
* **'er':** t-e-a-c-h-e-r (**teacher** 👩‍🏫), w-a-t-e-r (**water** 💧), f-l-o-w-e-r (**flower** 🌸).
* **'ir':** b-i-r-d (**bird** 🐦), g-i-r-l (**girl** 👧), s-h-i-r-t (**shirt** 👔), t-h-i-r-d (**third** 🥉).
* **'ur':** n-u-r-s-e (**nurse** 👩‍⚕️), t-u-r-t-l-e (**turtle** 🐢), p-u-r-p-l-e (**purple** 🟣), f-u-r (**fur** 🐈).
* **Story:** "The kind nurse gives water to the girl. A little bird has soft fur."

---

#### Week 23 — Grammar: Adjectives (Describing Words)
* **What is an Adjective?** A word that describes a Noun (color, size, number, quality, feeling).
* **Examples:**
  * *Color:* **red** apple, **blue** sky, **green** tree.
  * *Size:* **huge** elephant, **tiny** ant, **tall** giraffe.
  * *Taste/Touch:* **sweet** honey, **cold** ice cream, **soft** pillow.
  * *Character:* **kind** boy, **honest** merchant, **brave** soldier.
* **Sentence Practice:** "Bilal is an **honest** and **polite** boy."

---

#### Week 24 — Grammar: Prepositions of Place (in, on, under, behind, next to)
* **in:** "The pencil is **in** the pencil case."
* **on:** "The Quran is **on** the clean table."
* **under:** "The cat is sleeping **under** the bed."
* **behind:** "The sun is hiding **behind** the cloud."
* **next to / beside:** "Aisha is sitting **next to** her mother."
* **between:** "The ball is **between** two shoes."

---

#### Week 25 — Grammar: Conjunction 'and' & Opposites
* Joining sentences with 'and':
  * *I like apples. I like bananas.* -> *I like apples **and** bananas.*
  * *He is tall. He is strong.* -> *He is tall **and** strong.*
* **Opposites:** Clean 🧼 vs. Dirty 🧦, Heavy 🪨 vs. Light 🪶, Early 🌅 vs. Late 🌙, Rich 💎 vs. Poor 🤲.

---

#### Week 26 — Leveled Comprehension Story 2: "The Honest Woodcutter"
* **Text:**

  > *His iron axe slipped from his hand and fell into the deep water.*
  > *He was very sad because he had no money to buy a new axe.*
  > *An angel came out of the river with a shiny gold axe.*
  > *"Is this your axe?" asked the angel.*
  > *"No," said the honest man. "My axe is made of iron."*
  > *The angel was pleased with his honesty and gave him both gold and iron axes!*
* **Moral:** Honesty is always rewarded by Allah.
