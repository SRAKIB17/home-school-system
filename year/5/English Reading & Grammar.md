# English Reading & Grammar — Age 5 (Junior Level)

## 1. Learning Objectives
* Master Long Vowel Sounds (Magic 'e' rule & vowel teams like ee, ea, ai, oa).
* Learn 100+ High-Frequency Sight Words (Dolch Primer & First Grade words).
* Understand basic grammar: Nouns (Naming words), Verbs (Action words), Adjectives (Describing words), and Pronouns.
* Read leveled early reader storybooks independently with reading comprehension.
* Write simple 3–5 sentence paragraphs with correct capitalization and punctuation.
* Express thoughts and daily routines fluently in spoken English.

---

## 2. Long Vowels & Phonics Rules

### The Magic 'e' Rule (Silent 'e')
* **Long a_e:** cap -> cape, tap -> tape, mad -> made, cake, bake, lake.
* **Long i_e:** pin -> pine, kit -> kite, bite, ride, time, like.
* **Long o_e:** hop -> hope, rob -> robe, home, bone, rose.
* **Long u_e:** cub -> cube, tube, cute, mute.

### Vowel Teams & Diphthongs
* **ee & ea:** see, bee, tree, green / sea, tea, leaf, read, eat.
* **ai & ay:** rain, train, tail, wait / day, play, say, pray.
* **oa & ow:** boat, coat, road, soap / snow, blow, grow, window.
* **ou & ow:** out, shout, house / cow, clown, town.
* **R-Controlled Vowels:** car, star, park / corn, fork / bird, girl, nurse.

## 3. Core English Grammar

### Parts of Speech Foundations
1. **Nouns (Naming Words):**
   * Person: boy, girl, mother, teacher.
   * Place: school, home, park, mosque.
   * Thing: book, chair, pencil, table.
   * Animal: cat, bird, elephant, horse.
2. **Singular & Plural Nouns:**
   * Adding -s: book -> books, cat -> cats.
   * Adding -es: box -> boxes, bus -> buses, dish -> dishes.
3. **Pronouns (Replacing Nouns):**
   * I, He, She, It, We, They, You.
4. **Verbs (Action Words):**
   * run, jump, read, write, eat, drink, pray, smile.
5. **Helping Verbs (is, am, are):**
   * "I am a student." "He is kind." "They are playing."
6. **Adjectives (Describing Words):**
   * big, small, red, sweet, hot, kind, beautiful, sunny.

## 4. Reading Comprehension Stories

### Story 1: The Helpful Boy
* "Bilal is a good boy. He loves his parents. Every morning, he helps his mother water the plants. Then he feeds his pet bird. Bilal is kind and helpful."
* *Questions:* Who is Bilal? What does he do in the morning? Is he kind?

### Story 2: A Sunny Day in the Park
* "Today is a warm sunny day. The birds are singing in the green trees. Zain and his sister are playing with a red ball. They are very happy."

## 5. Guided Paragraph Writing & Spoken English
* **My Family (4 Sentences):**
  * "This is my family. My father is very kind. My mother makes yummy food. I love my family."
* **Spoken Dialogue:**
  * "What do you like to do?" -> "I like to read Islamic storybooks and ride my bicycle."
  * "How can I help you, Mama?" -> "Please bring my glasses, thank you!"
