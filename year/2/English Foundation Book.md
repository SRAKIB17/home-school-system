### 🅰️ Aa — /æ/ as in Apple & Allah
![A shiny juicy red apple with a green leaf and sweet smile](images/english/a_apple.png)

* **Letter Sound:** **Aa** says */æ/ - /æ/ - /æ/*
* **Keywords:** **Allah**, **Apple**, **Ant**
* **Sweet Rhyme:** 
  > 🍎 *"A is for Apple, sweet and round.*  
  > 🐜 *A is for Ant on the ground."*
* 👶 **Toddler Action:** Pretend to hold a big red apple and take a crunchy bite: *"Crunch! Mmm, yummy!"*

## 🏃 Chapter 2: Toddler Action Verbs & Fun Movements

![Happy toddler standing tall, clapping hands, and hugging mama](images/english/action_toddler.png)

1. 🧍 **Stand Up & Sit Down:**  
   > *"Stand up tall! (Reach for sky) ... Sit down small! (Sit on floor)"*
2. 👏 **Clap & Wave:**  
   > *"Clap, clap, clap your hands! Wave goodbye to your friends!"*
3. 🏃 **Run & Freeze:**  
   > *"Run, run, run, little baby... and FREEZE! (Stand super still)"*
4. 🤗 **Big Warm Hug:**  
   > *"Open your arms wide... Give Mama/Papa a BIG warm hug!"*

### 2. Baa Baa Black Sheep 🐑
