# English Foundation Book & Phonics — Age 2 (Foundation Level 1)

## 1. Learning Philosophy
* **Target Age:** 2 Years
* **Objectives:** Develop phonemic awareness (auditory letter sounds), object-word association, active listening, and early conversational vocabulary through cheerful interaction and nursery rhymes.

---

## 2. A to Z Phonics, Vocabulary & Action Rhymes

* **Aa /æ/ — Allah, Apple, Ant:**
  * "A is for Allah, Creator of all. A is for Apple, sweet and round."
  * *Action:* Pretend to hold an apple and take a crunchy bite: "Mmm, yummy!"
* **Bb /b/ — Bismillah, Ball, Baby:**
  * "B is for Bismillah before we start. B is for Ball, bounce bounce bounce."
  * *Action:* Bouncing an imaginary ball on the floor.
* **Cc /k/ — Cat, Cup, Car:**
  * "C is for Cat, purr purr meow. C is for Cup to drink warm milk."
  * *Action:* Stroke chin like a soft kitty and say "Meow!"
* **Dd /d/ — Dua, Duck, Door:**
  * "D is for Dua with open hands. D is for Duck, quack quack quack."
  * *Action:* Open two hands together for Dua.
* **Ee /e/ — Elephant, Egg, Eye:**
  * "E is for Elephant with a long nose. E is for Eyes to see the world."
  * *Action:* Point gently to the eyes and smile.
* **Ff /f/ — Family, Fish, Flower:**
  * "F is for Family we love so much. F is for Fish swimming in the sea."
  * *Action:* Wiggle hands like a little swimming fish.
* **Gg /g/ — Garden, Goat, Gift:**
  * "G is for Garden green and bright. G is for Gift wrapped so nice."
  * *Action:* Pretend to open a lovely gift box with a happy gasp!
* **Hh /h/ — Home, Hand, Hat:**
  * "H is for Home where we feel safe. H is for Hands to clap with joy."
  * *Action:* Clap hands rhythmically: 1, 2, 3!
* **Ii /ɪ/ — Islam, Insect, Igloo:**
  * "I is for Islam, our peaceful way. I is for Insect crawling by."
  * *Action:* Crawl fingers on the table like a tiny ant.
* **Jj /dʒ/ — Jannah, Jump, Juice:**
  * "J is for Jannah, our beautiful home. J is for Jump, up so high!"
  * *Action:* Take 2 small joyful jumps on the mat.
* **Kk /k/ — Kabah, Kite, Key:**
  * "K is for Kabah in Makkah city. K is for Kite flying in the sky."
  * *Action:* Wave hand in the air like a flying kite.
* **Ll /l/ — Light, Lion, Leaf:**
  * "L is for Light that shines so bright. L is for Lion, king of the forest."
  * *Action:* Gentle, cheerful lion roar: "Roar!"
* **Mm /m/ — Muhammad ﷺ, Moon, Milk:**
  * "M is for Muhammad, our beloved Prophet. M is for Moon in the night sky."
  * *Action:* Rub tummy gently: "Mmm, warm milk is good!"
* **Nn /n/ — Nose, Nest, Night:**
  * "N is for Nose to smell sweet flowers. N is for Night when we rest."
  * *Action:* Touch nose and take a deep breath in.
* **Oo /ɒ/ — Orange, Owl, Open:**
  * "O is for Orange, juicy and round. O is for Open, open the door!"
  * *Action:* Open arms wide and say "Open!"
* **Pp /p/ — Prayer, Pen, Pencil:**
  * "P is for Prayer five times a day. P is for Pencil to draw and write."
  * *Action:* Pretend to scribble on a paper with a pencil.
* **Qq /kw/ — Quran, Queen, Quick:**
  * "Q is for Quran, Allah's holy book. Q is for Quick, run to Mama!"
  * *Action:* Hold hands gently as if holding the holy Quran.
* **Rr /r/ — Rain, Rabbit, Red:**
  * "R is for Rain falling from clouds. R is for Rabbit, hop hop hop!"
  * *Action:* Wiggle two fingers on top of head like bunny ears.
* **Ss /s/ — Salam, Sun, Star:**
  * "S is for Salam to everyone we meet. S is for Sun shining warm and sweet."
  * *Action:* Wave cheerfully and say "As-Salamu Alaykum!"
* **Tt /t/ — Tree, Train, Table:**
  * "T is for Tree tall and green. T is for Train, choo choo choo!"
  * *Action:* Move arms back and forth like a train engine: "Choo-choo!"
* **Uu /ʌ/ — Umbrella, Up, Under:**
  * "U is for Umbrella in rainy days. U is for Up, look up to the sky!"
  * *Action:* Point up high with index finger.
* **Vv /v/ — Van, Vase, Vegetable:**
  * "V is for Van driving down the road. V is for Vegetables, crunchy and fresh."
  * *Action:* Hold imaginary steering wheel and drive.
* **Ww /w/ — Wudu, Water, Window:**
  * "W is for Wudu with clean cool water. W is for Window to see the morning."
  * *Action:* Rub hands together as if washing during Wudu.
* **Xx /ks/ — Xylophone, Box, Fox:**
  * "X is for Xylophone playing sweet notes. X is in Box and clever little Fox."
  * *Action:* Tap fingers on table like playing music keys.
* **Yy /j/ — Yellow, Yogurt, Yes:**
  * "Y is for Yellow, color of the sun. Y is for Yogurt, smooth and cold."
  * *Action:* Nod head gently and say "Yes, please!"
* **Zz /z/ — Zakat, Zebra, Zoo:**
  * "Z is for Zakat to help the poor. Z is for Zebra with black and white stripes."
  * *Action:* Make a gentle buzzing sound: "Zzzzz!"

## 3. Daily Action Words & Nursery Rhymes

### Action Verbs for Toddlers
* **Stand Up & Sit Down:** "Stand up tall, sit down small."
* **Clap & Wave:** "Clap your hands, wave goodbye."
* **Run & Stop:** "Run run run, and FREEZE/STOP!"
* **Smile & Hug:** "Give Mama a big warm hug!"

### Beloved Nursery Rhymes
1. **Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star:**
   * Twinkle, twinkle, little star, how I wonder what you are!
   * Up above the world so high, like a diamond in the sky.
2. **Baa Baa Black Sheep:**
   * Baa baa black sheep, have you any wool?
   * Yes sir, yes sir, three bags full!
3. **If You're Happy and You Know It:**
   * If you're happy and you know it, clap your hands! 👏 👏
   * If you're happy and you know it, say Alhamdulillah!
