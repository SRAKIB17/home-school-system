# English Focus — Age 2 (Foundation Level 1)

## Learning Philosophy
* **Age:** 2 Years
* **Focus:** Auditory familiarity with English alphabet sounds (Phonics), object association, vocabulary building, and basic communicative understanding through play.
* **Format:** Zero pressure, multi-sensory exposure, nursery rhymes, flashcards, and interactive games.

---

## 40-Week Curriculum Breakdown

### Part 1: Alphabet Sounds & Islamic / Familiar Objects (Weeks 1–13)
* **Week 1 — Letter A:** Sound /æ/ as in **Allah**, **Apple**, **Ant**. (Action: pretending to take a bite of an apple).
* **Week 2 — Letter B:** Sound /b/ as in **Ball**, **Baby**, **Bismillah**. (Action: bouncing a ball).
* **Week 3 — Letter C:** Sound /k/ as in **Cat**, **Car**, **Cup**. (Action: making a cat meow sound).
* **Week 4 — Letter D:** Sound /d/ as in **Duck**, **Door**, **Dua**. (Action: hands opened for Dua).
* **Week 5 — Letter E:** Sound /e/ as in **Egg**, **Elephant**, **Eye**. (Action: pointing to the eye).
* **Week 6 — Letter F:** Sound /f/ as in **Fish**, **Family**, **Flower**. (Action: swimming motion like a fish).
* **Week 7 — Letter G:** Sound /g/ as in **Garden**, **Goat**, **Gift**. (Action: giving a gift).
* **Week 8 — Letter H:** Sound /h/ as in **Home**, **Hand**, **Hat**. (Action: clapping hands).
* **Week 9 — Letter I:** Sound /ɪ/ as in **Islam**, **Insect**, **Igloo**. (Action: crawling fingers like an insect).
* **Week 10 — Letter J:** Sound /dʒ/ as in **Jannah**, **Jump**, **Juice**. (Action: taking small jumps).
* **Week 11 — Letter K:** Sound /k/ as in **Kabah**, **Kite**, **Key**. (Action: flying a kite).
* **Week 12 — Letter L:** Sound /l/ as in **Light**, **Lion**, **Leaf**. (Action: gentle roar like a lion).
* **Week 13 — Letter M:** Sound /m/ as in **Muhammad ﷺ**, **Moon**, **Milk**. (Action: rubbing tummy for yummy milk).

### Part 2: Letters N to Z & Vocabulary Building (Weeks 14–26)
* **Week 14 — Letter N:** Sound /n/ as in **Nose**, **Night**, **Nest**. (Action: touching nose).
* **Week 15 — Letter O:** Sound /ɒ/ as in **Orange**, **Owl**, **Open**. (Action: opening arms wide).
* **Week 16 — Letter P:** Sound /p/ as in **Prayer**, **Pen**, **Pencil**. (Action: making pencil scribbles).
* **Week 17 — Letter Q:** Sound /kw/ as in **Quran**, **Queen**, **Quick**. (Action: holding the Quran gently).
* **Week 18 — Letter R:** Sound /r/ as in **Rain**, **Rabbit**, **Red**. (Action: hopping like a rabbit).
* **Week 19 — Letter S:** Sound /s/ as in **Salam**, **Sun**, **Star**. (Action: smiling and saying Salam).
* **Week 20 — Letter T:** Sound /t/ as in **Tree**, **Train**, **Table**. (Action: moving arms like a train: choo-choo).
* **Week 21 — Letter U:** Sound /ʌ/ as in **Umbrella**, **Up**, **Under**. (Action: pointing up).
* **Week 22 — Letter V:** Sound /v/ as in **Van**, **Vegetable**, **Vase**. (Action: driving a van).
* **Week 23 — Letter W:** Sound /w/ as in **Water**, **Window**, **Wudu**. (Action: washing hands for Wudu).
* **Week 24 — Letter X:** Sound /ks/ as in **Xylophone**, **Box**, **Fox**. (Action: pretending to play xylophone).
* **Week 25 — Letter Y:** Sound /j/ as in **Yellow**, **Yogurt**, **Yes**. (Action: nodding head for "yes").
* **Week 26 — Letter Z:** Sound /z/ as in **Zakat**, **Zebra**, **Zoo**. (Action: making buzzing sound /z-z-z/).

### Part 3: Rhymes, Colors, Actions & Opposites (Weeks 27–34)
* **Week 27 — Rhymes & Action 1:** "Twinkle Twinkle Little Star" with hand actions.
* **Week 28 — Rhymes & Action 2:** "Baa Baa Black Sheep" & "Old MacDonald had a farm".
* **Week 29 — Basic Colors in English:** Red, Blue, Green, Yellow toy sorting.
* **Week 30 — Body Parts in English:** Head, Eyes, Nose, Mouth, Hands, Feet.
* **Week 31 — Action Verbs:** Stand up, Sit down, Clap, Stop, Smile, Hug.
* **Week 32 — Opposites Set 1:** Big vs. Small (Big elephant vs. Small ant).
* **Week 33 — Opposites Set 2:** Up vs. Down, Happy vs. Sad.
* **Week 34 — Family Words:** Mama, Papa, Brother, Sister, Baby.

### Part 4: Picture Talk, Interactive Review & Celebration (Weeks 35–40)
* **Week 35 — Picture Talk (Animals):** "What is this? It's a cat. What does the cat say? Meow!"
* **Week 36 — Picture Talk (Nature):** Looking at the sky, sun, clouds, and trees.
* **Week 37 — Review A–M:** Letter sound recognition with flashcard hunt game.
* **Week 38 — Review N–Z:** Matching letters with toy objects.
* **Week 39 — Favorite English Words:** Child selects and names their favorite pictures.
* **Week 40 — Joyful Review & Certificate:** Celebration of English sound recognition and speaking confidence.

---

## Daily Practice Tips for Parents
1. Use positive reinforcement: Clap, smile, and say "Good job!" or "Masha'Allah!"
2. Keep speaking English naturally in daily routines: "Drink water please", "Let's put on your shoes", "Look at the moon".
