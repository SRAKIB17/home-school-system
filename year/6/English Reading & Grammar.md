# English Reading & Grammar Master Textbook — Age 6 (Primary Level / Class 1)

## 1. Learning Philosophy & Objectives

### Philosophy & Vision
At age 6 (Class 1 / Primary Level), children transition into mature, expressive language mastery. English is taught not merely as a school subject, but as a global tool for critical thinking, literature appreciation, clear moral expression, and eloquent communication. This textbook integrates advanced grammar principles, leveled reading comprehension (Guided Reading Level D–I / Lexile 150L–350L), descriptive 5-to-7 sentence creative paragraph writing, and 3-minute public speaking confidence.

### Core Learning Milestones
1. **Advanced Parts of Speech & Sentence Syntax:**
   * Common vs. Proper Nouns, Singular/Plural (`-s, -es, -ies`, irregular plurals like *man/men, tooth/teeth*), Collective Nouns (*a flock of birds*).
   * Pronouns (Subject & Object Pronouns: *I/me, he/him, they/them*), Possessive Adjectives (*my, his, our, their*).
   * Tenses: Simple Present, Present Continuous (*verb + ing*), and Simple Past (*-ed*, irregular verbs like *go/went, eat/ate*).
   * Adjectives (Comparative & Superlative: *big/bigger/biggest*), Articles (*a, an, the*), Prepositions of Place and Time.
   * Conjunctions (*and, but, because, so, or*) and Sentence Types (Declarative, Interrogative, Exclamatory, Imperative).
2. **Reading Comprehension & Critical Analysis:** Understanding Main Idea, Characters, Setting, Conflict, Resolution, Inferences, and Sequencing (*First, Next, Then, Finally*).
3. **Structured Creative Composition:** Writing 5–7 sentence coherent descriptive, narrative, and letter-writing paragraphs.
4. **Vocabulary & Word Mechanics:** Compound words, Homophones (*see/sea, hear/here, son/sun*), Synonyms & Antonyms, Prefix/Suffix basics.
5. **Public Speaking & Debate:** Delivering 2–3 minute structured speeches and participating in respectful English discussions.

## 2. 40-Week Comprehensive English Reader & Grammar Curriculum

### Term 1: Grammar Foundations & Parts of Speech (Weeks 1–10)

---

#### Week 1 — Proper Nouns vs. Common Nouns & Capitalization
* **Common Nouns (General names of people, places, animals, things):** boy, girl, city, river, country, mosque, book, teacher.
* **Proper Nouns (Specific, unique names — Always capitalized!):** Bilal, Aisha, Dhaka, Nile River, Bangladesh, Baitul Mukarram, Holy Quran.
* **Capitalization Rules (M.I.N.T.S.):**
  * **M** - Months and days (January, Friday).
  * **I** - The pronoun "I" is always capitalized!
  * **N** - Names of people and places (Zayn, Madinah).
  * **T** - Titles of books and movies (The Lion King).
  * **S** - Start of every sentence.
* **Exercise:** Rewrite correctly: `yesterday i met bilal in dhaka on friday.` -> *Yesterday I met Bilal in Dhaka on Friday.*

---

#### Week 2 — Singular, Plural & Irregular Nouns
* **Rule 1 (Regular -s):** book -> books, pen -> pens, apple -> apples.
* **Rule 2 (-es after s, ss, sh, ch, x, z):** glass -> glasses, dish -> dishes, watch -> watches, fox -> foxes.
* **Rule 3 (-ies after consonant + y):** baby -> babies, story -> stories, fly -> flies, city -> cities. (Vowel + y just adds -s: boy -> boys, day -> days).
* **Rule 4 (Irregular Plurals — Change completely!):**
  * man -> **men** 👨‍👦
  * woman -> **women** 👩‍👧
  * child -> **children** 🧒
  * tooth -> **teeth** 🦷
  * foot -> **feet** 🦶
  * mouse -> **mice** 🐁
  * sheep -> **sheep** 🐑 (No change!)
  * fish -> **fish** 🐟 (No change!)

---

#### Week 3 — Personal Pronouns (Subject & Object Pronouns)
* **Subject Pronouns (Perform the action):** I, You, He, She, It, We, They.
  * *He* reads Quran every day. / *They* are playing soccer.
* **Object Pronouns (Receive the action):** me, you, him, her, it, us, them.
  * Mother gave *me* an apple. / Teacher called *him* to the board.
* **Practice Drills:** Replace nouns with correct pronouns in 5 sentences.

---

#### Week 4 — Possessive Adjectives & Possessive Nouns
* **Possessive Adjectives:** my, your, his, her, its, our, their.
  * "This is **my** bag." / "That is **her** pencil." / "We love **our** country."
* **Possessive Nouns (Apostrophe 's'):**
  * The book of Bilal -> **Bilal's book**.
  * The hat of the girl -> **The girl's hat**.
  * The nest of the bird -> **The bird's nest**.

---

#### Week 5 — Action Verbs & Subject-Verb Agreement
* **Rule:** A singular subject takes a singular verb (ends in -s/-es); a plural subject takes a base verb!
  * **He / She / It / Bilal** -> walk**s**, read**s**, play**s**, eat**s**.
  * **I / We / They / You / The boys** -> walk, read, play, eat.
* **Examples:**
  * *The bird sing**s** in the tree.* (Singular)
  * *The birds sing in the tree.* (Plural)
* **Error Correction:** "He run fast" ❌ -> *He runs fast* ✔️.

---

#### Week 6 — Helping Verbs: Am, Is, Are, Was, Were, Has, Have
* **Present Tense:**
  * **I am** a student.
  * **He / She / It is** happy.
  * **We / You / They are** friends.
* **Past Tense:**
  * **I / He / She / It was** at home yesterday.
  * **We / They / You were** at the park yesterday.
* **Has vs. Have:**
  * He / She / It **has** a red car.
  * I / We / They **have** new books.

---

#### Week 7 — Present Continuous Tense (Action Happening Now!)
* **Formula:** `Subject + is/am/are + Verb-ing`
* **Examples:**
  * I **am reading** an interesting Islamic storybook right now.
  * Look! The little sparrows **are flying** high in the blue sky.
  * Mother **is cooking** healthy soup in the kitchen.
* **Spelling Rules for -ing:**
  * swim -> swi**mm**ing, run -> ru**nn**ing (double consonant).
  * make -> mak**ing**, write -> writ**ing** (drop silent 'e').

---

#### Week 8 — Descriptive Adjectives & Sensory Words
* **What is an Adjective?** A word that describes a Noun through our 5 senses:
  * **Sight:** bright, dark, colorful, huge, tiny, circular.
  * **Sound:** loud, quiet, whispering, buzzing, melodious.
  * **Taste/Smell:** sweet, sour, fresh, fragrant, spicy.
  * **Touch:** soft, rough, smooth, hard, silky, cold.
  * **Feelings & Morals:** kind, truthful, brave, humble, generous.
* **Sentence Modeling:** "The **truthful** merchant sold **sweet, fresh** honey."

---

#### Week 9 — Comparative & Superlative Adjectives (-er & -est)
* **Positive -> Comparative (-er for 2) -> Superlative (-est for 3 or more):**
  * tall -> tall**er** -> tall**est** (Zayd is taller than Bilal; Umar is the tallest boy in class).
  * big -> bi**gg**er -> bi**gg**est
  * fast -> fast**er** -> fast**est**
  * sweet -> sweet**er** -> sweet**est**
* **Special Cases:** good -> **better** -> **best** 🌟.

---

### Term 2: Articles, Prepositions, Conjunctions & Syntax (Weeks 11–20)

---

#### Week 11 — The Definite & Indefinite Articles (A, An, The)
* **'A' (Indefinite — Consonant sound):** a boy, a university (starts with /juː/ sound), a one-eyed cat.
* **'An' (Indefinite — Vowel sound):** an apple, an hour (silent 'h' -> starts with /aʊ/ sound), an honest man.
* **'The' (Definite — Specific, unique, or previously mentioned things):**
  * *The* Holy Quran, *The* Sun, *The* Moon, *The* Atlantic Ocean.
  * "I saw a stray dog. **The** dog was very friendly."

---

#### Week 12 — Prepositions of Place & Spatial Navigation
* **Prepositions:** In, On, Under, Above, Below, Next to, Behind, In front of, Between, Among.
* **Examples:**
  * The lamp is hanging **above** the study table.
  * Bilal is standing **in front of** the class.
  * The cat is hiding **behind** the curtain.
  * The bird flew **through** the open window.

---

#### Week 13 — Prepositions of Time (At, On, In)
* **At (Exact clock time & night):** *at* 5:00 PM, *at* Fajr time, *at* night.
* **On (Specific days & dates):** *on* Friday, *on* 1st January, *on* my birthday.
* **In (Months, seasons, years, parts of the day):** *in* Ramadan, *in* summer, *in* 2026, *in* the morning.

---

#### Week 14 — Conjunctions: and, but, because, so, or
* **and (adds information):** "I bought a notebook **and** two pencils."
* **but (shows contrast):** "He is small, **but** he is very strong and brave."
* **because (gives reason):** "I went to bed early **because** I was tired."
* **so (shows result):** "It was raining, **so** we opened our umbrella."
* **or (gives choice):** "Would you like warm milk **or** fresh fruit juice?"

---

#### Week 15 — Question Words (The 5 Ws & 1 H)
* **Who:** for persons (*Who is your teacher?*)
* **What:** for things/actions (*What are you holding?*)
* **Where:** for places (*Where is the nearest mosque?*)
* **When:** for time (*When does the sun rise?*)
* **Why:** for reasons (*Why do we wash our hands?*)
* **How:** for method/manner (*How do birds fly?*)

---

#### Week 16 — The 4 Types of Sentences
1. **Declarative (Statement — ends with .):** *Honesty is the best policy.*
2. **Interrogative (Question — ends with ?):** *Did you complete your homework today?*
3. **Exclamatory (Strong emotion — ends with !):** *What a wonderful and blessed day!*
4. **Imperative (Command/Request — ends with . / !):** *Please close the door gently.*

---

#### Week 17 — Synonyms & Antonyms (Grade 1 Vocabulary)
* **Synonyms (Same meaning):**
  * start = begin | glad = happy | fast = quick | shut = close | neat = tidy | tiny = small.
* **Antonyms (Opposites):**
  * truth ❌ lie | generous ❌ stingy | brave ❌ cowardly | heavy ❌ light | full ❌ empty.

---

#### Week 18 — Homophones (Sound the Same, Spell Differently!)
* **see** (look 👀) vs. **sea** (ocean 🌊)
* **hear** (with ears 👂) vs. **here** (at this place 📍)
* **son** (boy child 👦) vs. **sun** (star in sky ☀️)
* **write** (with pen ✍️) vs. **right** (correct / direction ➡️)
* **two** (number 2) vs. **to** (direction) vs. **too** (also / very)
* **flour** (for baking bread 🍞) vs. **flower** (rose 🌹)

---

#### Week 19 — Compound Words & Word Building
* `sun` + `flower` = **sunflower** 🌻
* `rain` + `bow` = **rainbow** 🌈
* `tooth` + `brush` = **toothbrush** 🪥
* `butter` + `fly` = **butterfly** 🦋
* `foot` + `ball` = **football** ⚽

---

### Term 3: Leveled Reading Comprehension & Literary Analysis (Weeks 21–30)

---

#### Week 21 — Reading Comprehension 1: "The Honest Shepherd Boy"
* **Full Text:**
  > *A young shepherd named Tariq was grazing his sheep in the lush green valleys of Makkah.*
  > *The Caliph Umar (R.A.) passed by and wanted to test the boy's honesty.*
  > *"Sell me one of your fat sheep for a good price," said Umar.*
  > *Tariq replied, "They do not belong to me; they are my master's trust."*
  > *Umar said, "You can tell your master that a wolf ate it, and he will never know!"*
  > *Tariq looked up at the sky with deep awe and asked: "If my master does not see me, then where is Allah? (Fa Ayna Allah?)"*
  > *Tears rolled down Umar's cheeks. He was so moved by the boy's God-consciousness (Taqwa) that he purchased the entire flock and gifted it to the boy!*
* **Comprehension Questions:**
  1. What was Tariq doing in the valley?
  2. How did Caliph Umar (R.A.) test Tariq?
  3. What golden question did Tariq ask? (*Fa Ayna Allah?* — Where is Allah?)
  4. What moral character trait did the boy display?

---

#### Week 22 — Reading Comprehension 2: "The Secret Journey of a Tiny Seed"
* **Full Text:**
  > *A tiny acorn seed slept peacefully beneath the dark, cool earth.*
  > *When gentle spring rain soaked the ground, the seed woke up and drank the moisture.*
  > *It sent a small white root deep into the soil to hold on tight.*
  > *Then, a kiddy green shoot pushed through the brown dirt toward the warm sunlight.*
  > *Day after day, the leaves absorbed sunshine and carbon dioxide to make sweet nourishment.*
  > *Decades passed, and that tiny acorn transformed into a gigantic oak tree, sheltering hundreds of singing birds and tired travelers.*
* **Comprehension Questions:**
  1. What made the sleeping seed wake up?
  2. What did the seed send downward first? (A white root).
  3. How does the tree help animals and humans?

---

#### Week 23 — Reading Comprehension 3: "The Ant and the Thirsty Dove"
* **Full Text:**
  > *On a scorching hot summer afternoon, a little black ant was searching for water.*
  > *She crawled onto a blade of grass by the river, but slipped and fell into the rushing current!*
  > *A kind white dove sitting on a nearby branch saw the ant struggling.*
  > *The dove quickly plucked a broad green leaf and dropped it into the water near the ant.*
  > *The ant climbed onto the leaf and floated safely to the dry riverbank.*
  > *A few days later, a hunter aimed his net at the sleeping dove.*
  > *The ant saw the danger and bit the hunter hard on his heel!*
  > *The hunter screamed in pain and dropped his net. The dove flew away safely to the sky!*
* **Moral:** One good deed deserves another. Kindness is never wasted.

---

#### Week 24 — Reading Comprehension 4: "Prophet Muhammad's ﷺ Mercy"
* **Text & Moral:**
  > *An elderly woman in Makkah used to throw trash in the path of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ every day.*
  > *The Prophet ﷺ never got angry, never shouted, and never took revenge.*
  > *One day, the Prophet ﷺ noticed there was no trash on the path.*
  > *He inquired about her and learned she was severely ill in bed.*
  > *The Prophet ﷺ visited her home, greeted her with compassion, and offered to fetch water and medicine.*
  > *Overwhelmed by his noble character and forgiveness, the woman accepted Islam immediately.*

---

#### Week 25 — Story Analysis Matrix: Elements of a Story
* **1. Character (Who?):** The people or animals in the story.
* **2. Setting (Where & When?):** The location and time.
* **3. Problem / Conflict:** What went wrong?
* **4. Solution / Resolution:** How was the problem solved?
* **5. Moral / Lesson:** What did we learn from the story?

---

#### Week 26 — Sequencing Events: Chronological Order
* **Transition Signals:** **First** -> **Next** -> **Then** -> **After that** -> **Finally**.
* **Activity:** Read a scrambled 5-sentence story and arrange it in correct sequence (1 to 5).

---

#### Week 27 — Making Predictions & Drawing Inferences
* Reading between the lines: "Dark clouds filled the sky, lightning flashed, and mother brought in the dry clothes. What will happen next?" -> *It will rain heavily.*

### Term 4: Creative Composition, Letters & Public Speaking (Weeks 31–40)

---

#### Week 31 — Paragraph Writing 1: "My Daily Routine" (Chronological Paragraph)
* **Model Paragraph:**
  > *Every day begins early in the morning when the sweet Adhan of Fajr echoes.*
  > *First, I make Wudu with clean water and perform my prayer with my father.*
  > *Next, I recite the Holy Quran with proper Tajweed and eat a nutritious breakfast.*
  > *Then, I sit at my study desk to learn English, Mathematics, and Science.*
  > *In the afternoon, I play football in the garden and help my mother.*
  > *Finally, I read bedtime stories, say my evening Duas, and sleep peacefully.*

---

#### Week 32 — Paragraph Writing 2: "My Favorite Season — Spring"
* **Model Paragraph:**
  > *Spring is my absolute favorite season of the year.*
  > *During spring, the weather is pleasantly warm and breezy.*
  > *The trees sprout fresh green leaves, and colorful flowers blossom everywhere.*
  > *Butterflies dance around the sweet roses, and little birds sing joyful melodies.*
  > *I love walking in the green park with my family and thanking Allah for His marvelous creation.*

---

#### Week 33 — Paragraph Writing 3: "A Visit to the Beautiful Park" (Sensory Writing)
* **Sensory Framework:**
  * *Sight:* Tall eucalyptus trees, sparkling fountain, blooming sunflowers.
  * *Sound:* Chirping sparrows, laughing children, rustling leaves.
  * *Smell:* Fragrant jasmine, wet green grass.
  * *Touch:* Soft cool breeze, smooth wooden swing.
  * *Emotion:* Joyful, refreshed, grateful.

---

#### Week 34 — Picture Composition: Analyzing an Illustration
* Observing a detailed picture of a library or a family gardening scene and writing 6 connected sentences describing what is happening using Present Continuous Tense.

---

#### Week 35 — Friendly Letter & Thank-You Note Writing
* **Letter Format:**
  * **Date:** 18 August 2026
  * **Greeting:** *Dear Mother / Dear Teacher,*
  * **Body:** *Thank you so much for teaching me with immense patience and love. You always make learning fun and inspiring. I promise to work hard and make you proud.*
  * **Closing:** *With lots of love and respect,*
  * **Signature:** *[Child's Name]*

---

#### Week 36 — 3-Minute Public Speech: "How I Can Make the World a Better Place"
* **Speech Outline & Script:**
  > *"Honorable judges, beloved parents, and dear teachers, As-Salamu Alaykum!*
  > *Today, I stand before you to share how small hands can make a big difference in our world.*
  > *First, I can keep my environment clean by throwing trash into bins and planting shady trees.*
  > *Second, I can spread happiness by smiling at people, speaking the truth, and helping those in need.*
  > *Third, I can share my knowledge, food, and toys with others.*
  > *Our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us that even removing a harmful stone from the road is an act of charity.*
  > *Let us all be champions of kindness and peace! Thank you very much!"*
