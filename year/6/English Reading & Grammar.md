# English Reading & Grammar — Age 6 (Primary Level / Class 1)

## Learning Objectives
* **Age:** 6 Years
* **Target Competencies:**
  1. Fluent reading of Grade 1 leveled readers (Guided Reading Levels D–I / Lexile 100L–350L).
  2. Core English Grammar: Nouns, Verbs, Adjectives, Pronouns, Prepositions, Articles, and Simple Tenses.
  3. Sentence Structure: Identifying Subject + Verb + Object and writing coherent 5–7 sentence descriptive paragraphs.
  4. Reading Comprehension: Identifying the main idea, characters, setting, and sequence of events in stories.
  5. Spoken Communication: Giving 2–3 minute presentations, expressing opinions politely, and daily conversation.

---

## 40-Week Structured Curriculum

### Term 1: Grammar Foundations & Parts of Speech (Weeks 1–10)
* **Week 1 — Proper Nouns vs. Common Nouns:** 
  * Common: boy, city, book / Proper: Bilal, Dhaka, Quran (Capitalization rule for proper nouns).
* **Week 2 — Singular and Plural Nouns:** 
  * Adding -s, -es, and -ies (tree -> trees, bus -> buses, story -> stories). Irregular plurals (man -> men, tooth -> teeth).
* **Week 3 — Personal Pronouns:** I, He, She, It, We, They, You. (Subject pronouns).
* **Week 4 — Possessive Adjectives:** My, His, Her, Its, Our, Their, Your ("This is my bag. That is his pen.").
* **Week 5 — Action Verbs & Subject-Verb Agreement:** 
  * "He runs" vs. "They run". (Singular subject takes singular verb with -s).
* **Week 6 — Helping Verbs (Am, Is, Are, Was, Were):** Present vs. Past tense usage.
* **Week 7 — Present Continuous Tense (Verb + ing):** "The boy is reading", "The birds are singing".
* **Week 8 — Adjectives (Describing Words):** Color, Size, Shape, Number, Texture ("The fluffy white cat").
* **Week 9 — Comparative Adjectives:** big -> bigger -> biggest / tall -> taller -> tallest.
* **Week 10 — Term 1 Grammar Assessment & Mastery Quiz.**

### Term 2: Articles, Prepositions & Sentence Building (Weeks 11–20)
* **Week 11 — Articles (A, An, The):** 
  * Using 'A' before consonants, 'An' before vowel sounds, 'The' for specific or unique things.
* **Week 12 — Prepositions of Place:** In, On, Under, Above, Beside, Behind, In front of, Between.
* **Week 13 — Prepositions of Time:** At 6 o'clock, On Friday, In the morning.
* **Week 14 — Conjunctions (and, but, because, or):** 
  * "I like milk and honey", "He is small but strong", "I went home because it rained".
* **Week 15 — Question Words (Wh- Questions):** Who, What, Where, When, Why, How.
* **Week 16 — Types of Sentences:** Declarative (Statements .), Interrogative (Questions ?), Exclamatory (!).
* **Week 17 — Opposites (Antonyms) & Synonyms:** 30 essential Grade 1 vocabulary pairs.
* **Week 18 — Homophones:** see/sea, hear/here, son/sun, write/right, two/to.
* **Week 19 — Compound Words:** sun + flower = sunflower, rain + bow = rainbow, tooth + brush = toothbrush.
* **Week 20 — Mid-Year Grammar & Vocabulary Evaluation.**

### Term 3: Reading Comprehension & Guided Story Analysis (Weeks 21–30)
* **Week 21 — Reading Comprehension 1 (The Honest Shepherd):** Moral story, vocabulary and wh- questions.
* **Week 22 — Reading Comprehension 2 (The Journey of a Seed):** Science-based reading passage.
* **Week 23 — Reading Comprehension 3 (Animals in Winter):** Hibernation and migration concepts.
* **Week 24 — Reading Comprehension 4 (Prophet Muhammad's Kindness):** Story of forgiving the neighbor.
* **Week 25 — Story Elements Analysis:** Identifying Character, Setting (Where & When), Problem, and Solution.
* **Week 26 — Sequencing Events:** Using First, Next, Then, After that, and Finally to summarize stories.
* **Week 27 — Making Predictions & Inferences:** "What do you think will happen next?"
* **Week 28 — Rhyme & Poetry Recitation:** "The Wind" by R.L. Stevenson / Islamic poetry.
* **Week 29 — Independent Book Reading:** Reading a 10-page leveled storybook and giving an oral summary.
* **Week 30 — Term 3 Reading Fluency and Comprehension Exam.**

### Term 4: Creative Composition, Public Speaking & Presentation (Weeks 31–40)
* **Week 31 — Paragraph Writing 1 (My Daily Routine):** 
  * "I wake up early in the morning. I pray Fajr with my father. Then I brush my teeth and eat breakfast..."
* **Week 32 — Paragraph Writing 2 (My Favorite Season):** Describing weather, clothes, food, and feelings.
* **Week 33 — Paragraph Writing 3 (A Visit to the Zoo / Park):** Sensory details (sights, sounds, emotions).
* **Week 34 — Picture Composition:** Writing 5 connected sentences from a detailed illustration.
* **Week 35 — Writing a Friendly Thank-You Note:** Short letter to Parents or Teacher.
* **Week 36 — Public Speaking / Speech (2–3 minutes):** "How I Can Help My Community".
* **Week 37 — Spoken English Debate / Discussion:** "Why reading books is better than watching cartoons".
* **Week 38 — Dictation & Spelling Champion Test (40 words + 2 paragraphs).**
* **Week 39 — Annual English Language & Literature Assessment.**
* **Week 40 — "Primary English Scholar" Trophy & Certificate.**
