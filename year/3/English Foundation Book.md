# English Foundation Book & Phonics — Age 3 (Foundation Level 2)

## 1. Learning Philosophy & Objectives

### Philosophy & Vision
At age 3, language learning happens most effectively through multi-sensory auditory phonics, joyful rhyming songs, physical gestures (Total Physical Response - TPR), storybook reading, and natural daily conversational immersion. Rather than forcing rote memorization of letter names, this book equips the child with synthetic phonics sounds (A to Z), 3-letter CVC word blending, 20 essential sight words, daily spoken English manners, and Islamic values.

### Core Learning Milestones
1. **Letter-Sound Mastery (A–Z Phonics):** Instant recognition of uppercase and lowercase letters with their phonetic pure sounds and tactile gestures.
2. **CVC Word Blending:** Ability to blend individual sounds into 3-letter words (e.g., /c/ /æ/ /t/ -> *cat*).
3. **Decodable Readers & Sight Words:** Reading simple 3–4 word sentences fluently with confidence.
4. **Spoken English & Etiquette:** Using polite English phrases (*Please, Thank you, Excuse me, Good morning, As-Salamu Alaykum*) in daily routine.
5. **Fine-Motor Pre-Writing:** Tracing straight lines, curves, circles, and big letters with crayons.

## 2. 40-Week Comprehensive English Curriculum

### Term 1: Phonics Mastery A–M & Pre-Writing Strokes (Weeks 1–10)

---

#### Week 1 — Letter Aa: Pure Sound /æ/ & Action Rhyme
* **Phonics Sound:** /æ/ (Short 'a' as in *apple*).
* **Action Gesture:** Pretend to hold an apple in hand and take a crunchy bite: "Ah, ah, apple!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Apple 🍎, Ant 🐜, Axe 🪓, Airplane ✈️, Allah ﷻ.
* **Rhyme & Song:**
  > *"Ants on the apple, /æ/ - /æ/ - /æ/!*
  > *Ants on the apple, /æ/ - /æ/ - /æ/!*
  > *Sweet red apple, yum yum yum!"*
* **Parent-Child Dialogue:** "Look at the red apple! Who made this sweet apple for us? Allah did! Say Alhamdulillah!"
* **Pre-Writing:** Tracing standing and slanting lines (`/ \ -` for Capital A).

---

#### Week 2 — Letter Bb: Pure Sound /b/ & Action Rhyme
* **Phonics Sound:** /b/ (as in *ball*).
* **Action Gesture:** Pretend to bounce a rubber ball up and down: "Buh, buh, bounce!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Ball ⚽, Boy 👦, Bed 🛏️, Bird 🐦, Bismillah.
* **Sentence of the Week:** "The ball is blue."
* **Islamic Habit:** "Always say *Bismillah* before playing and eating!"

---

#### Week 3 — Letter Cc: Pure Sound /k/ & Whisker Action
* **Phonics Sound:** /k/ (as in *cat*).
* **Action Gesture:** Stroke imaginary whiskers on cheeks like a cute kitten: "K-k-cat!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Cat 🐱, Cup ☕, Car 🚗, Cake 🎂, Candle 🕯️.
* **Rhyme:**
  > *"We are clicking castanets, /k/ - /k/ - /k/!*
  > *Little cat in the car, /k/ - /k/ - /k/!"*
* **Daily Spoken Practice:** "May I have a cup of milk, please?"

---

#### Week 4 — Letter Dd: Pure Sound /d/ & Dua Action
* **Phonics Sound:** /d/ (as in *dog*).
* **Action Gesture:** Open both palms together in front of chest for Dua: "D-d-dua!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Dog 🐕, Duck 🦆, Door 🚪, Doll 🪆, Dua 🤲.
* **Parent-Child Dialogue:** "Let's make Dua to Allah! D is for Dua. We ask Allah to keep us healthy and happy."

---

#### Week 5 — Letter Ee: Pure Sound /e/ & Egg Cracking
* **Phonics Sound:** /e/ (Short 'e' as in *egg*).
* **Action Gesture:** Pretend to crack an egg on the side of a pan: "Eh, eh, egg!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Egg 🥚, Elephant 🐘, Engine 🚂, Elbow 💪.
* **Pre-Writing:** Tracing sleeping horizontal lines (`—`).

---

#### Week 6 — Letter Ff: Pure Sound /f/ & Swimming Fish
* **Phonics Sound:** /f/ (as in *fish*).
* **Action Gesture:** Put two palms together and make a swimming wave motion: "F-f-fish!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Fish 🐟, Fan 🪭, Frog 🐸, Fork 🍴, Family 👨‍👩‍👧.
* **Conversation:** "I love my sweet family!"

---

#### Week 7 — Letter Gg: Pure Sound /g/ & Gift Unwrapping
* **Phonics Sound:** /g/ (as in *goat*).
* **Action Gesture:** Pretend to open a ribbon on a gift box: "G-g-gift!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** Goat 🐐, Girl 👧, Grass 🌱, Gift 🎁, Garden 🏡.

---

#### Week 8 — Letter Hh: Pure Sound /h/ & Breathing on Palm
* **Phonics Sound:** /h/ (as in *house*).
* **Action Gesture:** Hold hand in front of mouth and feel the warm breath: "Huh, huh, hot!"
* **Vocabulary Words:** House 🏠, Hat 👒, Hen 🐔, Hand 🖐️, Heart ❤️.

---

#### Week 9 — Letters Ii & Jj: Sounds /ɪ/ & /dʒ/
* **Ii Sound /ɪ/:** Igloo 🧊, Ink 🖋️, Insect 🪲, Islam ☪️ (Wiggle fingers like crawling ants).
* **Jj Sound /dʒ/:** Jug 🥛, Jam 🍓, Jet ✈️, Jump 🦘, Jannah 🌴 (Take two happy little jumps).

---

#### Week 10 — Term 1 Phonics Review & Alphabet Sound Hunt
* Review sounds A through J with alphabet flashcards and sound bingo.
* Award: "Phonics Star Badge".

---

### Term 2: Phonics Mastery K–Z & Sound Blending (Weeks 11–20)

---

#### Week 11 — Letter Kk & Ll: Sounds /k/ & /l/
* **Kk /k/:** Kite 🪁, Key 🗝️, Kangaroo 🦘, Kabah 🕋 (Fly a kite in the air).
* **Ll /l/:** Lion 🦁, Leaf 🍃, Lemon 🍋, Lock 🔒, Light 💡 (Lick an ice cream and roar: "Lll-lion!").

---

#### Week 12 — Letter Mm & Nn: Sounds /m/ & /n/
* **Mm /m/:** Moon 🌙, Milk 🥛, Monkey 🐒, Mother 👩, Muhammad ﷺ (Rub tummy: "Mmm, delicious!").
* **Nn /n/:** Net 🕸️, Nest 🪺, Nut 🥜, Nose 👃, Night 🌃 (Touch nose and smile).

---

#### Week 13 — Letter Oo & Pp: Sounds /ɒ/ & /p/
* **Oo /ɒ/:** Orange 🍊, Owl 🦉, Ocean 🌊, On/Off 🔘 (Turn on and off light switch).
* **Pp /p/:** Pen 🖊️, Pot 🍲, Parrot 🦜, Prayer 🕌 (Pretend to write with a pen).

---

#### Week 14 — Letter Qq & Rr: Sounds /kw/ & /r/
* **Qq /kw/:** Quran 📖, Queen 👑, Quiet 🤫 (Finger on lips: "Shh, quiet!").
* **Rr /r/:** Rain 🌧️, Rabbit 🐇, Ring 💍, Rose 🌹 (Hop like a cute bunny).

---

#### Week 15 — Letter Ss & Tt: Sounds /s/ & /t/
* **Ss /s/:** Sun ☀️, Star ⭐, Spoon 🥄, Snake 🐍, Salam 🤝 (Slither hand like snake: "S-s-sun!").
* **Tt /t/:** Tree 🌳, Table 🪑, Train 🚂, Tiger 🐯, Toy 🧸 (Chug arms like train: "Choo-choo!").

---

#### Week 16 — Letter Uu & Vv: Sounds /ʌ/ & /v/
* **Uu /ʌ/:** Umbrella ☂️, Up ⬆️, Under ⬇️, Uncle 👨 (Open imaginary umbrella over head).
* **Vv /v/:** Van 🚐, Vase 🏺, Violin 🎻, Vegetables 🥕 (Hold steering wheel of van).

---

#### Week 17 — Letters Ww, Xx, Yy, Zz
* **Ww /w/:** Watch ⌚, Water 💧, Window 🪟, Wudu 🫧 (Rub hands making Wudu).
* **Xx /ks/:** Box 📦, Fox 🦊, Six 6️⃣, X-ray 🩻 (Cross arms like an X).
* **Yy /j/:** Yellow 💛, Yo-yo 🪀, Yes 👍, Yacht ⛵ (Nod head vigorously: "Yes!").
* **Zz /z/:** Zebra 🦓, Zoo 🦁, Zip 🤐, Zero 0️⃣, Zakat 💰 (Buzz like a bumblebee: "Zzzzz!").

---

#### Week 18 — 26 Letters Alphabet Song & Upper-Lower Case Match
* Matching capital letters with small letters (`A -> a`, `B -> b`, `C -> c` ... `Z -> z`).

---

#### Week 19 — The 5 Magic Vowels: A, E, I, O, U
* Introduce the 5 singing vowel friends that give voice to all words.

---

#### Week 20 — Mid-Year Phonics Celebration & Reading Readiness
* Complete oral sound assessment across all 26 letters.

---

### Term 3: CVC Word Families & Early Blending (Weeks 21–30)

---

#### Week 21 — Short 'a' -at Family Blending
* **Sound Formula:** Consonant + /æ/ + /t/
* **Words:**
  * c - a - t -> **cat** 🐱
  * b - a - t -> **bat** 🏏
  * m - a - t -> **mat** 🧘
  * h - a - t -> **hat** 🎩
  * r - a - t -> **rat** 🐀
* **Mini Reader:** "The **cat** sat on the **mat**. The **cat** saw a **rat**!"

---

#### Week 22 — Short 'a' -an and -ap Families
* **-an Family:** p - a - n -> **pan** 🍳, f - a - n -> **fan** 🪭, m - a - n -> **man** 👨, v - a - n -> **van** 🚐.
* **-ap Family:** c - a - p -> **cap** 🧢, m - a - p -> **map** 🗺️, t - a - p -> **tap** 🚰.
* **Mini Reader:** "A **man** has a **cap**. The **man** is in the **van**."

---

#### Week 23 — Short 'e' -ed and -en Families
* **-ed Family:** b - e - d -> **bed** 🛏️, r - e - d -> **red** 🔴.
* **-en Family:** h - e - n -> **hen** 🐔, p - e - n -> **pen** 🖊️, t - e - n -> **ten** 🔟.
* **Mini Reader:** "I see a **red** **hen**. The **hen** is on the **bed**."

---

#### Week 24 — Short 'i' -in and -ig Families
* **-in Family:** p - i - n -> **pin** 📌, b - i - n -> **bin** 🗑️, w - i - n -> **win** 🏆.
* **-ig Family:** b - i - g -> **big** 🐘, p - i - g -> **pig** 🐷, d - i - g -> **dig** ⛏️.
* **Mini Reader:** "A **big** **pig** can **dig**."

---

#### Week 25 — Short 'o' -og and -ot Families
* **-og Family:** d - o - g -> **dog** 🐕, l - o - g -> **log** 🪵, f - o - g -> **fog** 🌫️.
* **-ot Family:** h - o - t -> **hot** ☕, p - o - t -> **pot** 🍲, d - o - t -> **dot** 🔴.
* **Mini Reader:** "The **dog** is on the **log**. The **pot** is **hot**."

---

#### Week 26 — Short 'u' -un and -ug Families
* **-un Family:** s - u - n -> **sun** ☀️, r - u - n -> **run** 🏃, b - u - n -> **bun** 🍞.
* **-ug Family:** m - u - g -> **mug** 🍺, b - u - g -> **bug** 🐞, h - u - g -> **hug** 🫂.
* **Mini Reader:** "The **bug** is on the **mug**. I **run** in the **sun**."

---

#### Week 27 — CVC Word Slider Game
* Move paper slider to change first letter: `b-at`, `c-at`, `f-at`, `h-at`, `m-at`.

---

#### Week 28 — Rhyming Words Match
* Which words sound the same? (cat - bat, red - bed, sun - run, dog - log).

---

#### Week 29 — Color Words Recognition
* Red 🔴, Blue 🔵, Green 🟢, Yellow 🟡, Black ⚫, White ⚪, Pink 🌸.

---

#### Week 30 — Term 3 CVC Reading Showcase
* Child reads 10 CVC word flashcards independently.

---

### Term 4: Sight Words, Early Readers & Spoken English (Weeks 31–40)

---

#### Week 31 — Core Sight Words Group 1 (I, see, a, the)
* **Target Sight Words:** **I**, **see**, **a**, **the**.
* **Story 1:**
  * "I see a cat."
  * "I see a dog."
  * "I see the sun."

---

#### Week 32 — Core Sight Words Group 2 (is, in, on, my)
* **Target Sight Words:** **is**, **in**, **on**, **my**.
* **Story 2:**
  * "My cat is on the mat."
  * "My pen is in the cup."
  * "My ball is red."

---

#### Week 33 — Core Sight Words Group 3 (can, like, go, to)
* **Target Sight Words:** **can**, **like**, **go**, **to**.
* **Story 3:**
  * "I like my mom."
  * "I can run."
  * "I go to the park."

---

#### Week 34 — Daily Spoken Routine 1: Waking Up & Greetings
* **Parent:** "Good morning, sweetheart! Did you sleep well?"
* **Child:** "Good morning, Mom! Alhamdulillah, I slept well!"
* **Action:** Brushing teeth together and saying "Bismillah".

---

#### Week 35 — Daily Spoken Routine 2: Table Manners & Sharing
* **Child:** "May I have some bread, please?"
* **Parent:** "Here you are, my dear!"
* **Child:** "Thank you, Dad!"
* **Parent:** "You are very welcome!"

---

#### Week 36 — Daily Spoken Routine 3: Asking for Help & Feelings
* "Mom, please help me with my shoes."
* "I am very happy today!" / "I am a little tired."

---

#### Week 37 — Decodable Storybook: "A Fun Day in the Sun"
* Full 4-page mini story read by the child:
  > *Page 1: The sun is hot.*
  > *Page 2: The big dog can run.*
  > *Page 3: The cat has a red hat.*
  > *Page 4: We have fun in the sun!*
