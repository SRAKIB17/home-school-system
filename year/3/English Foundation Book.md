# English Foundation Book & Phonics — Age 3 (Foundation Level 2)

## 1. Learning Objectives
* Master letter-sound correspondence (Synthetic Phonics A–Z).
* Early CVC sound blending (e.g., /c/-/æ/-/t/ -> cat).
* Recognize and understand 100+ common everyday English words.
* Understand simple English sentences and commands.
* Practice tracing lines, curves, and uppercase/lowercase letters.

---

## 2. A to Z Phonics & CVC Word Families

### Unit 1: Phonics Sounds (A–M)
* **Aa /æ/:** Apple, Ant, Axe, Airplane.
* **Bb /b/:** Ball, Boy, Bed, Bird.
* **Cc /k/:** Cat, Cup, Car, Cake.
* **Dd /d/:** Dog, Duck, Door, Doll.
* **Ee /e/:** Elephant, Egg, Engine, Eye.
* **Ff /f/:** Fish, Fan, Frog, Fork.
* **Gg /g/:** Goat, Girl, Grass, Gift.
* **Hh /h/:** House, Hat, Hen, Hand.
* **Ii /ɪ/:** Igloo, Ink, Insect, Island.
* **Jj /dʒ/:** Jug, Jam, Jet, Jump.
* **Kk /k/:** King, Kite, Key, Kangaroo.
* **Ll /l/:** Lion, Leaf, Lemon, Lock.
* **Mm /m/:** Moon, Monkey, Milk, Mother.

### Unit 2: Phonics Sounds (N–Z)
* **Nn /n/:** Net, Nest, Nut, Nose.
* **Oo /ɒ/:** Orange, Owl, Ocean, On.
* **Pp /p/:** Pen, Pencil, Pot, Parrott.
* **Qq /kw/:** Quran, Queen, Quill, Quiet.
* **Rr /r/:** Rain, Rabbit, Ring, Red.

* **Zz /z/:** Zebra, Zoo, Zip, Zero.

## 3. CVC Word Families (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant)

### Short 'a' Family
* **-at:** c-a-t (cat), b-a-t (bat), m-a-t (mat), h-a-t (hat), r-a-t (rat).
* **-an:** p-a-n (pan), m-a-n (man), v-a-n (van), f-a-n (fan).
* **-ap:** c-a-p (cap), m-a-p (map), t-a-p (tap).

### Short 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u' Families
* **Short 'e':** b-e-d (bed), r-e-d (red), h-e-n (hen), p-e-n (pen).
* **Short 'i':** p-i-n (pin), b-i-g (big), p-i-g (pig), s-i-t (sit).
* **Short 'o':** d-o-g (dog), h-o-t (hot), p-o-t (pot), b-o-x (box).
* **Short 'u':** s-u-n (sun), r-u-n (run), c-u-p (cup), m-u-g (mug).

## 4. Sight Words & Simple Sentences
* **Early Sight Words:** I, am, see, a, the, like, is, in, on.
* **Reading Sentences:**
  * "I see a cat."
  * "The cat is on the mat."
  * "The sun is hot."
  * "I like my red pen."
  * "This is a big dog."

---

## 5. Spoken English Dialogues
* "Good morning! How are you?" -> "I am fine, Alhamdulillah!"
* "What is your name?" -> "My name is ..."
* "May I have some water, please?" -> "Thank you!" -> "You are welcome!"
