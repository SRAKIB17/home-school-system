# English Foundation Book & Phonics — Age 3 (Foundation Level 2)

## 1. Learning Philosophy & Objectives
* **Age:** 3 Years (Foundation Level 2)
* **Goal:** Mastery of A–Z synthetic phonics sounds, early 3-letter CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant) blending, auditory recognition of 100+ words, pre-writing tracing strokes, and speaking simple daily conversational sentences.

## 3. CVC Word Families & Early Sound Blending

### Short 'a' Word Families
* **-at Family:** c-a-t -> **cat**, b-a-t -> **bat**, m-a-t -> **mat**, h-a-t -> **hat**, r-a-t -> **rat**.
* **-an Family:** p-a-n -> **pan**, f-a-n -> **fan**, m-a-n -> **man**, v-a-n -> **van**, c-a-n -> **can**.
* **-ap Family:** c-a-p -> **cap**, m-a-p -> **map**, t-a-p -> **tap**, n-a-p -> **nap**.

### Short 'e', 'i', 'o', 'u' Families
* **Short 'e':** b-e-d -> **bed**, r-e-d -> **red**, h-e-n -> **hen**, p-e-n -> **pen**, t-e-n -> **ten**.
* **Short 'i':** p-i-n -> **pin**, b-i-g -> **big**, p-i-g -> **pig**, s-i-t -> **sit**, w-i-n -> **win**.
* **Short 'o':** d-o-g -> **dog**, h-o-t -> **hot**, p-o-t -> **pot**, b-o-x -> **box**, f-o-x -> **fox**.
* **Short 'u':** s-u-n -> **sun**, r-u-n -> **run**, c-u-p -> **cup**, m-u-g -> **mug**, b-u-s -> **bus**.

## 4. Sight Words & Decodable Early Sentences
* **Core Sight Words:** I, a, see, the, my, is, in, on, can, like.
* **Reading Sentences:**
  1. "I see a cat."
  2. "The cat is on the mat."
  3. "A red hen is in the pen."
  4. "The big dog can run."
  5. "I see the hot sun."
  6. "I like my cup."

---

## 5. Daily Spoken English Dialogues at Home
* **Greeting & Manners:**
  * Parent: "Good morning, my sweet child!"
  * Child: "Good morning, Mama! As-Salamu Alaykum!"
* **Polite Requests:**
  * "May I have some water, please?" -> "Here you go!" -> "Thank you!" -> "You are welcome!"
* **Expressing Joy:**
  * "I did it! Alhamdulillah!"
