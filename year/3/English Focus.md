# English Focus — Age 3 (Foundation Level 2)

## Goals & Philosophy
* **Age:** 3 Years
* **Objectives:** 
  1. Master letter-sound correspondence (Synthetic Phonics A–Z).
  2. Early CVC blending intro (e.g., /c/-/æ/-/t/ -> cat).
  3. Recognize and say 100+ common English vocabulary words.
  4. Understand and respond to simple daily English instructions & questions.
  5. Develop pencil grip through tracing lines, curves, and uppercase/lowercase letters.

---

## 40-Week Curriculum Plan

### Unit 1: Phonics Sounds (A–M) & Letter Recognition (Weeks 1–13)
* **Week 1 — Letter A:** /æ/ — Apple, Ant, Airplane, Allah. (Tracing: standing/slanting lines).
* **Week 2 — Letter B:** /b/ — Ball, Boy, Bed, Bird. (Tracing: vertical lines and loops).
* **Week 3 — Letter C:** /k/ — Cat, Cup, Car, Cake. (Tracing: left curves).
* **Week 4 — Letter D:** /d/ — Dog, Duck, Door, Doll. (Tracing: straight line + big curve).
* **Week 5 — Letter E:** /e/ — Elephant, Egg, Engine, Eye. (Action: sound blending exercise).
* **Week 6 — Letter F:** /f/ — Fish, Fan, Frog, Fork. (Tracing practice).
* **Week 7 — Letter G:** /g/ — Goat, Girl, Grass, Gift. (Object hunt in house).
* **Week 8 — Letter H:** /h/ — House, Hat, Hen, Hand. (Simple greeting: "Hello!").
* **Week 9 — Letter I:** /ɪ/ — Igloo, Ink, Insect, Island.
* **Week 10 — Letter J:** /dʒ/ — Jug, Jam, Jet, Jump. (Action: Jump 3 times!).
* **Week 11 — Letter K:** /k/ — King, Kite, Key, Kangaroo.
* **Week 12 — Letter L:** /l/ — Lion, Leaf, Lemon, Lock.
* **Week 13 — Letter M:** /m/ — Moon, Monkey, Milk, Mother. (Phonics review A–M).

### Unit 2: Phonics Sounds (N–Z) & Basic Tracing (Weeks 14–26)
* **Week 14 — Letter N:** /n/ — Net, Nest, Nut, Nose.
* **Week 15 — Letter O:** /ɒ/ — Orange, Owl, Ocean, On/Off.
* **Week 16 — Letter P:** /p/ — Pen, Pencil, Pot, Parrott.
* **Week 17 — Letter Q:** /kw/ — Quran, Queen, Quill, Quiet ("Shh!").
* **Week 18 — Letter R:** /r/ — Rain, Rabbit, Ring, Red.
* **Week 19 — Letter S:** /s/ — Sun, Star, Spoon, Snake. (S-curve tracing).
* **Week 20 — Mid-Year Phonics Carnival & Review (Letters A–S).**
* **Week 21 — Letter T:** /t/ — Table, Tree, Tiger, Top.
* **Week 22 — Letter U:** /ʌ/ — Umbrella, Up, Under, Uncle.
* **Week 23 — Letter V:** /v/ — Van, Vase, Violin, Vest.
* **Week 24 — Letter W:** /w/ — Watch, Water, Window, Wall.
* **Week 25 — Letter X & Y:** /ks/ & /j/ — Box, Fox, Xylophone / Yellow, Yo-yo, Yacht.
* **Week 26 — Letter Z:** /z/ — Zebra, Zoo, Zip, Zero. (Complete A–Z Sound Drill).

### Unit 3: Phonics Blending (CVC Words) & Early Sight Words (Weeks 27–34)
* **Week 27 — Short 'a' Family (-at words):** c-a-t -> cat, b-a-t -> bat, m-a-t -> mat.
* **Week 28 — Short 'a' Family (-an words):** p-a-n -> pan, m-a-n -> man, v-a-n -> van.
* **Week 29 — Short 'e' & 'i' Words:** b-e-d -> bed, r-e-d -> red, p-i-n -> pin, b-i-g -> big.
* **Week 30 — Short 'o' & 'u' Words:** d-o-g -> dog, h-o-t -> hot, s-u-n -> sun, c-u-p -> cup.
* **Week 31 — Early Sight Words:** I, am, see, a, the, like, is. (Reading: "I see a cat", "The sun is hot").
* **Week 32 — Colors & Shapes in English:** Red circle, Blue square, Green triangle, Yellow star.
* **Week 33 — Daily Objects in English:** Spoon, Plate, Glass, Chair, Pillow, Blanket.
* **Week 34 — Family & Community Helpers:** Teacher, Doctor, Driver, Farmer.

### Unit 4: Spoken English, Nursery Rhymes & Conversation (Weeks 35–40)
* **Week 35 — Spoken Dialogue 1 (Greetings):**
  * "Good morning! How are you?" -> "I am fine, thank you / Alhamdulillah!"
* **Week 36 — Spoken Dialogue 2 (Needs & Polite words):**
  * "May I have water, please?" -> "Thank you!" -> "You are welcome!"
* **Week 37 — English Nursery Rhymes:**
  * "Humpty Dumpty", "The Wheels on the Bus", "Incy Wincy Spider".
* **Week 38 — Picture Talk & Storytelling:**
  * Describing a park scene: "The boy is playing with a ball. The bird is on the tree."
* **Week 39 — Full Phonics & Vocabulary Master Review:**
  * Interactive flashcards, object hunt, and sound puzzles.
* **Week 40 — Celebration & Achievement Certification:**
  * Cheerful storytelling presentation and award.

## Daily Home-Schooling Practice
* **Word of the Day:** Introduce 1 new English word each day during breakfast or play.
* **Read-Aloud (Storytime):** 10 minutes before bedtime, read a colorful picture book pointing at words and pictures.
